Overview
--------

**GEOMETOR Kepler** (``geometor-kepler``) unites a **4-Track Bilingual Digital Scriptorium Book Codex** (`English`, `Latin`, `Summary`, `Notes`) with **100% exact symbolic SymPy / ``geometor.model`` constructions and proofs** across the three foundational geometric treatises of :person:`Johannes Kepler <kepler>` (1571–1630):

1. ***Mysterium Cosmographicum* (1596 / 1621)**: Kepler's July 19, 1595 Graz discovery nesting the :term:`five regular Platonic solids <corpora-quinque-regularia>` between the six heliocentric planetary orbs of :person:`Nicolaus Copernicus <copernicus>`, proving polar-duality insphere/circumsphere equality ($\text{Cube} = \text{Octahedron}$ with $Q_{\text{in}}/Q_{\text{out}} = \frac{1}{3}$; $\text{Dodecahedron} = \text{Icosahedron}$ with $Q_{\text{in}}/Q_{\text{out}} = \frac{5 + 2\sqrt{5}}{15}$) and celebrating the *Two Great Treasures of Geometry*—the Pythagorean Theorem and the :term:`Divine Section <sectio-divina>` $\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$.
2. ***Strena seu de Nive Sexangula* (*On the Six-Cornered Snowflake*, 1611)**: Dedicated to :person:`Johannes Matthäus Wackher von Wackenfels <wackher>`, founding mathematical crystallography and natural morphology: discovery of the space-filling :term:`Rhombic Dodecahedron <dodecaedron-rhombicum>` ($\sqrt{2}:1$) and golden :term:`Rhombic Triacontahedron <triacontaedron-rhombicum>` ($\phi:1$), the **Kepler Sphere-Packing Conjecture** ($\frac{\pi}{3\sqrt{2}} \approx 74.048\%$), and the self-propagating Fibonacci recurrence $1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21\dots \to \phi$ in pentamerous blossoms.
3. ***Harmonices Mundi Libri V* (*The Harmony of the World*, 1619 — Books I, II & V) & the 1597 Kepler Right Triangle**: Euclidean degrees of :term:`geometric knowability <scibilitas>` vs. the irreducible cubic :term:`non-constructibility <inscibilitas>` of the regular heptagon ($x^3 + x^2 - 2x - 1 = 0$); complete classification of the **11 uniform Archimedean planar tilings**, pentagonal/decagonal star tessellation (`Figure Aa`), the **13 Archimedean convex solids**, and the **Small & Great Stellated Dodecahedra** ($\{5/2, 5\}, \{5/2, 3\}$); the :term:`Kepler Right Triangle <kepler-triangle>` $(1, \sqrt{\phi}, \phi)$ over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\phi})$; planetary aphelion/perihelion consonances; and **Kepler's Harmonic Third Law** $(T_1/T_2)^2 = (a_1/a_2)^3$.
