===============
Johannes Kepler
===============

.. image:: _static/medallion.svg
   :alt: Johannes Kepler Medallion
   :class: repo-medallion-hero

:person:`Johannes Kepler <kepler>` (1571–1630) united geometry, music, and astronomy into a single search for the architecture of the cosmos—nesting the five Platonic solids between Copernican planetary orbs in 1596, asking why winter snowflakes always crystallize with six-fold symmetry in 1611, and unveiling the Third Law of planetary motion alongside the polyphonic *Music of the Spheres* in 1619. This edition presents all three Latin treatises in synchronized four-track Book Codices (*Latin · English · Summary · Notes*) paired with **616 archival folio scans**, interactive 3D CAD reconstructions, and a live Just-Intonation celestial music studio.

Interactive Studios & Key Features
----------------------------------

.. container:: home-feature-grid

   .. container:: home-feature-card

      **Live Celestial Music & Just-Intonation Studio**

      Hear Kepler's seven engraved planetary melodies (folio ``0299``), ignite the elliptical **Keplerian Orbital Orrery Choir**, strike all **Four Universal Creation Chords** (folios ``0301``–``0302``), and pluck the **1596/1619 Vibrating Brass Monochord** inside the :ref:`Celestial Music Studio <harmonices-audio-studio>` (`Full-Screen Studio <_static/harmonices-mundi-studio.html>`_, `Planetary Motet .MP3 <_static/audio/kepler_planetary_motet.mp3>`_, `Creation Chords .MP3 <_static/audio/kepler_creation_chords.mp3>`_, and `Just-Intonation .MIDI <_static/audio/kepler_harmonices_1619.mid>`_).

   .. container:: home-feature-card

      **Tabula III 3D Parametric CAD Studio**

      Rotate Christoph Leibfried's 1597 copperplate in full cutaway 3D—six hemispherical planetary bowls, five open-frame Platonic scaffolds, glowing Solar core, and Renaissance pedestal—in the :ref:`3D CAD Studio <mysterium-3d-studio>` (`Full-Screen WebGL <_static/tabula-iii-3d.html>`_, native `.IGZ <_static/models/tabula_iii.igz>`_ / `.GLB <_static/models/tabula_iii.glb>`_ / `.OBJ <_static/models/tabula_iii.obj>`_) beside :ref:`Chapter II <mc-cap_02-en>` and :ref:`Chapter XIV <mc-cap_14-en>`.

   .. container:: home-feature-card

      **3D Star Polyhedra & 616 Archival Folios**

      Spin the Small and Great Stellated Dodecahedra ($\{5/2, 5\}$ and $\{5/2, 3\}$) beside Kepler's 1619 engravings in :ref:`Book II, Prop. XXVI <hm-liber-ii-prop_26-en>`, and compare original 1596/1611/1619 copperplates with interactive ``.euclid-graphic`` vectorizations beginning in :ref:`Chapter I <mc-cap_01-en>`.
