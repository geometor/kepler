Preface: Summary
================

Core Discovery of the Graz Chalkboard (July 19, 1595)
-----------------------------------------------------

**Dimensional Shift from Infinite Plane Polygons to Five Convex Solids**: While teaching the zodiacal progression of Saturn–Jupiter great conjunctions at Graz on July 19, 1595, :person:`Kepler <kepler>` observed that the incircle and circumcircle of an equilateral triangle stand in ratio :math:`1 : 2` (:math:`Q_{\text{in}}/Q_{\text{out}} = 1/4`). Realizing that regular 2D polygons form an infinite family whereas three-dimensional Euclidean space admits **strictly five** regular convex polyhedra (:just:`XIII.18`), he deduced an *a priori* geometric explanation for why :person:`Copernicus's <copernicus>` heliocentric system possesses exactly **six** mobile planets separated by **five** interplanetary intervals.
