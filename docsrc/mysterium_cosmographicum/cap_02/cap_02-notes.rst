Chapter II: Historical & Mathematical Commentary
================================================

Nicholas of Cusa & The Geometrical Trinity
------------------------------------------

Kepler's theological geometry draws explicitly on :person:`Nicholas of Cusa's <cusanus>` *De Docta Ignorantia* (:cite:`1440 <cusanus:1440>`), where the infinite sphere is a symbol of the divine essence. For Kepler, the central Sun represents God the Father, the outer stellar sphere God the Son, and the intermediate celestial medium the Holy Spirit. Rectilinear solids act as the archetypal scaffolding through which divine ideas are instantiated in physical magnitude.
