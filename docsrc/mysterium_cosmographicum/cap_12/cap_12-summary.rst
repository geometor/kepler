Chapter XII: Summary
====================

The Two Great Treasures of Geometry
-----------------------------------

1. **Measure of Gold (*Massa Auri*)**: The Pythagorean Theorem (:just:`I.47`), governing the Cube, Tetrahedron, and Octahedron over $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})$.
2. **Precious Jewel (*Gemma*)**: Division in Extreme and Mean Ratio (:term:`sectio divina <sectio-divina>`, $\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$, :just:`II.11`, :just:`VI.30`), governing the regular pentagon, Dodecahedron, and Icosahedron over $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$.
3. **Polygonal & Harmonic Correspondence**: Marginal diagrams on p. 42 (1596) connect regular and star polygon circle divisions ($n = 2, 4, 5, 6, 5/2, 8/3$) to harmonic intervals and planetary aspects.
