Chapter XVI: Notes & Provenance
===============================

Abolition of Crystalline Spheres & Physical Space
-------------------------------------------------

Chapter XVI resolves a common caricature of the *Mysterium Cosmographicum*: Kepler never imagined physical crystal cubes or brass dodecahedra grinding in the sky. Just as he later cites :person:`Tycho Brahe <brahe>` in Chapter XXII (``0090``), Kepler already treats the Platonic polyhedra here on folios ``0065``–``0068`` as purely formal spatial constraints—preparing the conceptual space for free-moving planetary bodies driven by a central solar force in Chapter XX.
