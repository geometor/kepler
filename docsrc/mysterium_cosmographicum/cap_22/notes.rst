Chapter XXII: Notes & Provenance
================================

The Direct Seed of Kepler's Second Law (Equal Areas)
----------------------------------------------------

Folio ``0090`` (p. 78) is arguably the single most prophetic page in the entire *Mysterium Cosmographicum*. Notice three historic milestones converging on one page: (1) Kepler praises :person:`Tycho Brahe <brahe>` by name for dissolving solid celestial spheres; (2) he replaces kinematic equants/epicycles with a **physical radial force law** centered at the true Sun :math:`A` (inverse-distance speed law :math:`v \propto 1/r`, which is exact at aphelion and perihelion for an ellipse under conservation of angular momentum!); and (3) he flags Earth's missing equant as an unresolved anomaly—the very clue that guided his war on Mars in *Astronomia Nova* (:cite:`1609 <kepler:1609:an>`).

In the 1596 woodcut on folio ``0090`` (p. 78), the three circles are labeled ``IKPQ`` (outer, with ``R`` and ``S`` at the sides), ``EFGH`` (middle), and ``NOLM`` (inner); on p. 79 (folio ``0091``), Kepler occasionally refers to the inner and outer circles as ``NOPQ`` and ``IKLM`` in the prose—both letterings are preserved verbatim in the Latin and English tracks while linked to the calibrated vector diagram ``0090.g1.svg``.
