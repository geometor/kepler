Chapter XIX: Summary
====================

Individual Planetary Residuals & Mästlin's Critique of Copernicus
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Kepler audits Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Venus, and Mercury individually, showing how Saturn's vast distance cushions its radial shift and Jupiter matches to ``6'``, while quoting Michael Mästlin's sharp technical diagnosis of Venus and Mercury: Copernicus borrowed ancient Ptolemaic circle dimensions despite a changing terrestrial eccentricity.
