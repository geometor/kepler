Chapter V: Summary
==================

The Cube as Archetypal Generator & Unique Regular Space-Filler
--------------------------------------------------------------

Kepler assigns the Cube (``{4,3}``) to the outermost planetary gap between Saturn and Jupiter because it holds geometric primacy among the three primary solids: it alone is generated directly by translating its square base, it serves as the seed from which both the Tetrahedron (by truncation) and Dodecahedron (by roof construction, Euclid XIII.17) are built, and it is the sole Platonic solid that tiles 3D space without gaps.
