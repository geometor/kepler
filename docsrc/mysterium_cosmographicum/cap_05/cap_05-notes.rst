Chapter V: Notes & Provenance
=============================

Cube Construction of the Tetrahedron & Dodecahedron
---------------------------------------------------

Kepler's remark on folio ``0038`` (p. 28) about building the Dodecahedron by affixing six roof-like *pentahedra* (hipped wedges with two pentagon-halves and two triangles) onto the faces of an inscribed Cube refers directly to :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` celebrated construction in *Elements* XIII.17 (:cite:`Euclid <euclid:elements>`). Likewise, slicing four orthogonal corner tetrahedra off a Cube leaves the regular inscribed Tetrahedron whose edge is the Cube's face diagonal ($\sqrt{2}$), anticipating Kepler's exact sphere-ratio computations in Chapter XIII.
