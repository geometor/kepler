Christian Frisch
================

German philologist, mathematician, and editor of the eight-volume critical *Joannis Kepleri Astronomi Opera Omnia* (Frankfurt and Erlangen: Heyder & Zimmer, 1858–1871), supplying the authoritative Latin source texts for this digital edition.
