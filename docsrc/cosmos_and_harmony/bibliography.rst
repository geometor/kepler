Bibliography
============

.. rubric:: Primary Editions, Classical Sources & Reference Literature

Primary Public-Domain Editions of Johannes Kepler
-------------------------------------------------

.. _cite-kepler-1596-mc:

* **Kepler (1596)** — Johannes Kepler, *Prodromus Dissertationum Cosmographicarum, continens Mysterium Cosmographicum, de admirabili proportione orbium coelestium*, Tübingen: Georgius Gruppenbachius, 1596 (ETH-Bibliothek Zürich Rar 1371 / e-rara-445). *(Editio princeps, with appendix and orbital calculations by Michael Mästlin; Public Domain.)*

.. _cite-kepler-1621-mc2:

* **Kepler (1621)** — Johannes Kepler, *Prodromus Dissertationum Cosmographicarum, continens Mysterium Cosmographicum (Editio altera cum notis authoris)*, Frankfurt am Main: Erasmus Kempfer / Godefridus Tampachius, 1621. *(Author's annotated second edition linking the 1596 treatise to Astronomia Nova and Harmonices Mundi; Public Domain.)*

.. _cite-kepler-1597-maestlin:

* **Kepler (1597)** — Johannes Kepler, *Epistola ad Michaelem Mästlinum* (Graz, October 1597), in *Joannis Kepleri Astronomi Opera Omnia*, ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. I (1858), p. 377. *(Formulates the Kepler Right Triangle $1 : \sqrt{\phi} : \phi$; Public Domain.)*

.. _cite-kepler-1609-an:

* **Kepler (1609)** — Johannes Kepler, *Astronomia Nova ΑΙΤΙΟΛΟΓΗΤΟΣ, seu Physica Coelestis, tradita commentariis de Motibus Stellae Martis*, Heidelberg: Gotthard Vögelin, 1609. *(Establishes Kepler's First and Second Laws of focal elliptical planetary motion; Public Domain.)*

.. _cite-kepler-1611-strena:

* **Kepler (1611)** — Johannes Kepler, *Ioannis Kepleri S. Cæs. Maiest. Mathematici Strena seu de Nive Sexangula*, Frankfurt am Main: Godefridus Tampachius, 1611 (24 pp.). *(Editio princeps of On the Six-Cornered Snowflake; Public Domain.)*

.. _cite-kepler-1619-hm:

* **Kepler (1619)** — Johannes Kepler, *Ioannis Keppleri Harmonices Mundi Libri V*, Linz: Godofredus Tampachius / excudebat Ioannes Plancus, 1619 ([8], 66, 255 pp., 5 engraved plates). *(Editio princeps of The Harmony of the World; Public Domain.)*

.. _cite-frisch-1858-v1:

* **Frisch (1858, Vol. I)** — Christian Frisch (ed.), *Joannis Kepleri Astronomi Opera Omnia, Volumen I*, Frankfurt a. M. & Erlangen: Heyder & Zimmer, 1858, pp. 95–214, 377. *(Critical Latin edition of Mysterium Cosmographicum 1596/1621 and the Mästlin correspondence; Public Domain.)*

.. _cite-frisch-1864-v5:

* **Frisch (1864, Vol. V)** — Christian Frisch (ed.), *Joannis Kepleri Astronomi Opera Omnia, Volumen V*, Frankfurt a. M. & Erlangen: Heyder & Zimmer, 1864, pp. 75–334. *(Critical Latin edition of Harmonices Mundi Libri V; Public Domain.)*

.. _cite-frisch-1868-v7:

* **Frisch (1868, Vol. VII)** — Christian Frisch (ed.), *Joannis Kepleri Astronomi Opera Omnia, Volumen VII*, Frankfurt a. M. & Erlangen: Heyder & Zimmer, 1868, pp. 715–730. *(Critical Latin edition of Strena seu de Nive Sexangula; Public Domain.)*

Classical, Medieval & Renaissance Authorities
---------------------------------------------

.. _cite-euclid-elements:

* **Euclid** — *Stoicheia* (*Elements*, ed. J. L. Heiberg / trans. T. L. Heath), Books I, II, IV, VI, X, XIII; Digital Scriptorium ``geometor/euclid`` & ``geometor/elements``.

.. _cite-proclus-in-euclidem:

* **Proclus Diadochus** — *In Primum Euclidis Elementorum Librum Commentarii* (ed. G. Friedlein, Leipzig: Teubner, 1873); Digital Scriptorium ``geometor/proclus``.

.. _cite-pappus-synagoge-v:

* **Pappus of Alexandria** — *Synagoge* (*Mathematical Collection*), Book V, Props. 1–19 (Honeycomb planimetry & the 13 Archimedean semi-regular solids); ``geometor/pappus``.

.. _cite-cusanus-1440:

* **Nicolaus Cusanus** — *De Docta Ignorantia Libri Tres* (1440), Book I, Chs. 11–21 (Curved and Straight as theological-mathematical exemplars).

.. _cite-pacioli-1509:

* **Luca Pacioli** — *De Divina Proportione*, Venice: Paganino Paganini, 1509; Digital Scriptorium ``geometor/pacioli``.

.. _cite-duerer-1525:

* **Albrecht Dürer** — *Underweysung der Messung, mit dem Zirckel und Richtscheyt*, Nuremberg, 1525.

.. _cite-rheticus-1540:

* **Georg Joachim Rheticus** — *Narratio Prima* (Danzig, 1540; reprinted by Michael Mästlin in Kepler's 1596 *Mysterium Cosmographicum*).

.. _cite-copernicus-1543:

* **Nicolaus Copernicus** — *De revolutionibus orbium coelestium Libri VI*, Nuremberg: Johannes Petreius, 1543.
