Conspectus Operis
-----------------

Spanning twenty-five years from his youthful flash of insight at Graz on July 19, 1595 to the Linz *Harmonices Mundi* (1619) and Frankfurt annotated *Mysterium* (1621), :person:`Johannes Kepler <kepler>` forged the decisive bridge between classical Greek synthetic geometry (:person:`Euclid <euclid>`, :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>`, :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>`, :person:`Ptolemy <ptolemy>`, :person:`Pappus <pappus>`, :person:`Proclus <proclus>`) and modern mathematical physics, crystallography, and algebra. Each unit of this **Four-Track Bilingual Book Codex** provides synchronized reading tabs (**English**, **Latin**, **Summary**, and **Notes**) wired to interactive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVG figures:

* **Unit I** (*Mysterium Cosmographicum · 1596 / 1621; Frisch I, pp. 106–152*): :doc:`Mysterium Cosmographicum: Five Regular Solids & the Six Planetary Orbs <unit-1-mysterium-cosmographicum/unit-1-mysterium-cosmographicum-en>` — The July 19, 1595 Graz Great-Conjunction triangle ($r_{\text{in}} : R_{\text{out}} = 1 : 2$), :person:`Nicholas of Cusa's <cusanus>` theology of curved and straight, the *Two Great Treasures of Geometry* (Pythagoras & the :term:`Divine Section <sectio-divina>`), polar-duality insphere/circumsphere equality ($\text{Cube} = \text{Octahedron}$ with $Q = \frac{1}{3}$; $\text{Dodecahedron} = \text{Icosahedron}$ with $Q = \frac{5 + 2\sqrt{5}}{15}$), and *Terra* as :term:`Circulus mensor omnium <circulus-mensor>`. (:doc:`EN <unit-1-mysterium-cosmographicum/unit-1-mysterium-cosmographicum-en>` · :doc:`LA <unit-1-mysterium-cosmographicum/unit-1-mysterium-cosmographicum-la>` · :doc:`Summary <unit-1-mysterium-cosmographicum/unit-1-mysterium-cosmographicum-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <unit-1-mysterium-cosmographicum/unit-1-mysterium-cosmographicum-notes>`)
* **Unit II** (*Strena seu de Nive Sexangula · 1611; Frisch VII, pp. 715–730*): :doc:`Strena: Six-Cornered Snowflakes, Rhombic Polyhedra, Sphere Packing & Fibonacci <unit-2-strena-snowflake/unit-2-strena-snowflake-en>` — Crossing the Prague bridge, trihedral bee honeycomb bases, discovery of the space-filling :term:`Rhombic Dodecahedron <dodecaedron-rhombicum>` ($\sqrt{2}:1$, dihedral $120^\circ$) and golden :term:`Rhombic Triacontahedron <triacontaedron-rhombicum>` ($\phi:1$), pomegranate compression and the :term:`Kepler Sphere-Packing Conjecture <kepler-conjecture>` ($\frac{\pi}{3\sqrt{2}} \approx 74.048\%$), and self-propagating Fibonacci pentamery ($1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21 \to \phi$). (:doc:`EN <unit-2-strena-snowflake/unit-2-strena-snowflake-en>` · :doc:`LA <unit-2-strena-snowflake/unit-2-strena-snowflake-la>` · :doc:`Summary <unit-2-strena-snowflake/unit-2-strena-snowflake-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <unit-2-strena-snowflake/unit-2-strena-snowflake-notes>`)
* **Unit III** (*Harmonices Mundi Liber I · 1619; Frisch V, pp. 75–125*): :doc:`Harmonices Mundi Book I: Geometric Scibilitas, Divine Section & the Heptagon <unit-3-harmonices-liber-i/unit-3-harmonices-liber-i-en>` — Defense of :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` Book X against :person:`Petrus Ramus <ramus>` and :person:`Willebrord Snellius <snellius>`, the eight degrees of :term:`geometric knowability <scibilitas>` (*effabilis longitudine*, *effabilis potentia*, *mese*, *binominis & apotome*, *mizon & elasson*), self-propagation of the :term:`Sectio Divina <sectio-divina>`, and Proposition XLV proving the Euclidean :term:`non-constructibility <inscibilitas>` of the regular heptagon ($x^3 + x^2 - 2x - 1 = 0$) against :person:`Jost Bürgi's <buergi>` cossic chord equation. * **Unit IV** (*Harmonices Mundi Liber II · 1619; Frisch V, pp. 126–144*): :doc:`Harmonices Mundi Book II: Architectonic Congruence, Tilings & Star Polyhedra <unit-4-harmonices-liber-ii/unit-4-harmonices-liber-ii-en>` — Planar and spatial :term:`congruence <congruentia>` ($\sum \theta_i = 360^\circ$), complete enumeration of the **11 uniform Archimedean planar tilings**, Kepler's pentagonal/decagonal star tessellation (`Figure Aa`, precursor to :person:`Roger Penrose's <penrose>` aperiodic tilings), reconstruction of all **13 Archimedean convex solids**, and discovery of the **Small & Great Stellated Dodecahedra** ($\{5/2, 5\}$ and $\{5/2, 3\}$). * **Unit V** (*Epistola 1597 & Harmonices Mundi Liber V · 1619; Frisch I p. 377, V pp. 269–329*): :doc:`Harmonices Mundi Book V: The Kepler Triangle, Celestial Consonances & Third Law <unit-5-harmonices-liber-v/unit-5-harmonices-liber-v-en>` — The October 1597 letter to :person:`Michael Mästlin <maestlin>` formulating the :term:`Kepler Right Triangle <kepler-triangle>` $(1, \sqrt{\phi}, \phi)$ over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\phi})$, harmonic marriage of the five regular solids with :person:`Tycho Brahe's <brahe>` orbital eccentricities, :term:`aphelion/perihelion musical consonances <harmonia-extremorum>` ($4:5, 5:6, 2:3, 15:16, 24:25, 5:12$), and **Kepler's Harmonic Third Law** (:term:`proportio sesquialtera <lex-tertia>`, $(T_1/T_2)^2 = (a_1/a_2)^3$). Editorial & Translation Note
----------------------------

All primary Neo-Latin texts in this codex are edited from the 1596, 1611, 1619, and 1621 *editiones principes* and :person:`Christian Frisch's <frisch>` critical *Joannis Kepleri Astronomi Opera Omnia* (:cite:`1858 <frisch:1858:v1>`, :cite:`1864 <frisch:1864:v5>`, :cite:`1868 <frisch:1868:v7>`). Every English translation (`-en`), geometric executive summary (`-summary`), and scholarly commentary (`-notes`) is **100% originally authored for GEOMETOR** directly from Kepler's Latin; no modern copyrighted translations were consulted or reproduced.
