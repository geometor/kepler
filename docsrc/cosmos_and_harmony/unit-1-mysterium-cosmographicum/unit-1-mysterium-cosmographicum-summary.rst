Unit I · Executive Geometric Summary
====================================

Core Theses of *Mysterium Cosmographicum* (1596 / 1621)
-------------------------------------------------------

1. **Dimensional Shift from Infinite Plane Polygons to Five Convex Solids**: While teaching the zodiacal progression of Saturn–Jupiter great conjunctions at Graz on July 19, 1595, :person:`Kepler <kepler>` observed that the incircle and circumcircle of an equilateral triangle stand in ratio $1 : 2$ ($Q_{\text{in}}/Q_{\text{out}} = 1/4$). Realizing that regular 2D polygons form an infinite family whereas three-dimensional Euclidean space admits **strictly five** regular convex polyhedra (:just:`XIII.18`), he deduced an *a priori* geometric explanation for why :person:`Copernicus's <copernicus>` heliocentric system possesses exactly **six** mobile planets separated by **five** interplanetary intervals.
2. **The Two Great Treasures of Geometry**: In Chapter XII, Kepler formulates his enduring aphorism pairing the **Pythagorean Theorem** (*measure of gold*, governing the Cube, Tetrahedron, and Octahedron over $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})$) with the **Extreme and Mean Ratio** (:term:`sectio divina <sectio-divina>` $\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$, *precious jewel*, governing the Dodecahedron and Icosahedron over $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$).
3. **Polar-Duality Insphere/Circumsphere Invariance**: Dual regular solids exchange vertices ($V$) and faces ($F$) while preserving edge count ($E$) and exact insphere-to-circumsphere quadrance ratios:

4. **Earth as Measuring Boundary (*Circulus mensor omnium*)**: Placing Earth ($R_\oplus = 1$) between the three outer trihedral *primary* solids (Dodecahedron, Tetrahedron, Cube) and the two inner *secondary* solids (Icosahedron, Octahedron) reproduces the qualitative spacing of the six classical planets—especially the vast Jovian gap ($1/3$ tetrahedral ratio between Mars and Jupiter) and tight terrestrial cluster ($0.795$ golden ratios around Earth and Venus).
