Chapter IV: Difference Between Book III Music & Book IV Planetary Aspects
=========================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0218
   :page: pp. 128–132

Crucially, why do **Book IV planetary aspects** permit intervals such as **Quintile** ($72^\circ = \frac{1}{5}$), **Biquintile** ($144^\circ = \frac{2}{5}$), and **Sesquiquadrate** ($135^\circ = \frac{3}{8}$), whereas Book III rejects $1:5$ as a standalone melodic step? Because in music we divide a *linear string* (parts vs. parts), whereas on the zodiac **two rays converge at Earth's center**, comparing each arc directly against the **entire circle** ($360^\circ$)!
