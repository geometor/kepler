Chapter IV: Summary
===================

Establishes Kepler's inverse-square extremal angular-speed theorem $\omega_{\text{per}}/\omega_{\text{aph}} = ((1+e)/(1-e))^2$ and defines the harmonic comparison between adjacent planets' converging and diverging speeds.
