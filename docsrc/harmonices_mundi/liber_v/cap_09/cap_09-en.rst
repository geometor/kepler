Chapter IX: Polyhedral Synthesis of Orbital Eccentricities & Solar Epilogue
===========================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0306
   :page: pp. 215–255

In **Chapter IX**—comprising Propositions I through XLVII—Kepler solves the grand synthesis uniting *Mysterium Cosmographicum* (1596) with *Harmonices Mundi* (1619). Why did the :term:`five regular solids <corpora-quinque-regularia>` alone leave small orbital residuals? Because God tuned the planetary spheres simultaneously to **two archetypes**: the inscribed regular solids establish approximate inter-sphere spacing, while the **just-intonation musical consonances** of extremal motions lock the exact eccentricities $e = \frac{\sqrt{q} - \sqrt{p}}{\sqrt{q} + \sqrt{p}}$ (for Mars's fifth $2:3$, $e = 5 - 2\sqrt{6} \approx 0.1010$). The book closes with Kepler's rapturous hymn to the Sun and cosmic harmony (*Epilogus de Sole*, pp. 251–255).
