Chapter IX: Summary
===================

Completes Kepler's joint polyhedral-harmonic proof across 49 propositions (Props. I–XLIX, pp. 214–243), deriving orbital eccentricities :math:`e` analytically from consonant aphelion/perihelion ratios, comparing predicted intervals against Tycho Brahe's observations, and closing with the *Epiphonema* and hymn of thanksgiving, verified in ``verify_kepler_third_law()``.
