Caput III Prop. VIII: Historical & Mathematical Commentary
==========================================================

From *Proportio Sesquialtera* (1618) to Universal Gravitation (1687)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Kepler's Third Law $(T_1 / T_2)^2 = (a_1 / a_2)^3$ (*Book V*, Ch. III, Prop. VIII) became the decisive quantitative touchstone from which Isaac Newton deduced in *Principia* (1687, Book I, Props. IV & XI) that a centripetal force holding planets in focal ellipses with $T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2}{GM} a^3$ must vary **inversely as the square of the distance** ($F \propto 1/r^2$). Two years after *Harmonices Mundi*, Kepler brought his lifework full circle by issuing the annotated second edition of *Mysterium Cosmographicum* (:cite:`Frankfurt 1621 <kepler:1621:mc2>`), leaving his youthful 1596 text intact while supplying candid *Notae Authoris* explaining where pure polyhedral geometry required completion by elliptical dynamics and celestial harmony.
