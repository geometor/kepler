Definitions I–XXV: Summary
==========================

Cyclotomic Minimal Polynomials of $2\cos(2\pi/n)$
--------------------------------------------------

In ``geometor.kepler.harmonices_geometry.classify_polygon_scibilitas``, every regular $n$-gon studied by Kepler is classified by its exact minimal polynomial over $\mathbb{Z}$:

.. list-table:: Scibilia vs. Inscibilia in *Harmonices Mundi* Book I
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 8 24 28 10 30

   * - $n$
     - Polygon
     - Minimal Polynomial of $x = 2\cos(2\pi/n)$
     - Deg
     - Kepler's Book I Classification
   * - $3$
     - Equilateral Triangle
     - $x + 1 = 0$ ($s_3^2 = 3R^2$)
     - $1$
     - **III.** *Effabilis potentiâ tantùm*
   * - $4$
     - Square
     - $x = 0$ ($s_4^2 = 2R^2$)
     - $1$
     - **III.** *Effabilis potentiâ tantùm*
   * - $5$
     - Regular Pentagon
     - $x^2 + x - 1 = 0$
     - $2$
     - **VIII.** *Elasson* / *Mizon* over $\phi$
   * - $6$
     - Regular Hexagon
     - $x - 1 = 0$ ($s_6 = R$)
     - $1$
     - **II.** *Effabilis longitudine*
   * - **$7$**
     - **Regular Heptagon**
     - **$x^3 + x^2 - 2x - 1 = 0$**
     - **$3$**
     - ***Inscibilis*** *(Prop. XLV cubic)*
   * - $8$
     - Regular Octagon
     - $x^2 - 2 = 0$
     - $2$
     - **VI–VIII.** Bisected square
   * - **$9$**
     - **Regular Nonagon**
     - **$x^3 - 3x + 1 = 0$**
     - **$3$**
     - ***Inscibilis*** *(Trisected trigon)*
   * - $10$
     - Regular Decagon
     - $x^2 - x - 1 = 0$
     - $2$
     - **VI.** *Apotome* ($s_{10} = R/\phi$)
   * - **$11$**
     - **Regular Hendecagon**
     - **$x^5 + x^4 - 4x^3 - 3x^2 + 3x + 1 = 0$**
     - **$5$**
     - ***Inscibilis*** *(Irreducible quintic)*
   * - $12$
     - Regular Dodecagon
     - $x^2 - 3 = 0$
     - $2$
     - **VI–VIII.** Bisected hexagon
   * - $15$
     - Regular Pentadecagon
     - $x^4 - x^3 - 4x^2 + 4x + 1 = 0$
     - $4$
     - **VIII.** Compounded $3$- & $5$-gon
   * - $16$
     - Regular Hexadecagon
     - $x^4 - 4x^2 + 2 = 0$
     - $4$
     - **VIII.** Iterated quadratic tower
