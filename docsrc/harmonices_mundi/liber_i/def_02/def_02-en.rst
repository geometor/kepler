Definition II: Primary vs. Augmented (Star) Figures
===================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0023
   :page: p. 6

Of these, some are **primary and radical**, which do not go beyond their own bounds, to which the definition just laid down properly belongs; others are **augmented**, which go as it were beyond their own sides, when non-adjacent sides of some radical figure are continued to their intersection: these are called **Stars**.

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0025
   :page: p. 7

Thus here :math:`ABCDE` is a complete pentagon, and it is a primary figure, requiring no other complete figure from which it may be produced by continuing the sides.

Whereas :math:`FGHIKE` is a pentagonal star, and an augmented figure, formed by continuing pairs of non-adjacent sides—for instance :math:`AB` and :math:`DC`—to their intersection :math:`I`.
