Definition III: Semi-Regular Figures (Rhombi)
=============================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0024
   :page: p. 7

**Semi-regular** are those which, varying in their angles, have four equal sides; as the Rhombi :math:`NMPO`, :math:`GEKD`.
