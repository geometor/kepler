Proposition XXIV: Summary
=========================

Demonstrates the algebraic reciprocity $(x+y)^2 = x^2 + y^2 + 2xy$ and $(x-y)^2 = x^2 + y^2 - 2xy$ geometrically with an inscribed subdivided square on folio ``0032`` (p. 14).
