Proposition XXIII: Notes & Provenance
=====================================

Middle of folio ``0031`` (p. 13).

In Euclid's *Elements* Book X, Propositions 71, 108, and 109 address irrational magnitudes arising from combining medial areas with expressible or medial areas, showing why pairs of *Mesae* (too low in degree) or mixed Expressible/*Mese* pairs fail to yield the sixth-degree species.
