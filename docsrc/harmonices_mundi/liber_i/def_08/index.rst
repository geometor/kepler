Definition VIII: Knowable (Scibile)
===================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0025
   :page: p. 8

**Knowable** (:term:`scibile <scibilitas>`) is said of that which is either immediately measurable through itself by the diameter, if it is a line, or by its square, if it is a surface; or which is formed at least from such quantities by a definite and geometrical ratio, which—however long the series—nevertheless depends at last upon the Diameter or its square. In Greek it is called γνώριμον (*gnorimon*).
