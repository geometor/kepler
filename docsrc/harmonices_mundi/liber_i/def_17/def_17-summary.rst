Definition XVII: Summary
========================

In Degree V, symmetric invariants $x^2 + y^2$ and $xy$ both lie in $\mathbb{Q} \cdot D^2$, allowing nested radical sides $(x \pm y)$ to reduce cleanly to Binomials and Apotomes.
