Definition XIV: Third Degree (Expressible in Power Only)
========================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0027
   :page: p. 10

The **Third Degree** is this: when a line is Inexpressible in length, while its square is Expressible and belongs to the second degree. It is called ῥητὴ δυνάμει, **Expressible in power** (:term:`effabilis potentiâ tantùm <effabilis-potentia>`).
