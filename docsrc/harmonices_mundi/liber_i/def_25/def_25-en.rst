Definition XXV: Mizon, Elasson, and Origin of Euclid's Twelve Species
=====================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0031
   :page: p. 14

From the second pairing of the sixth degree (Definition XVIII: sum of squares expressible, rectangle *Meson*), addition produces the **Mizon** or **Major**, and subtraction the **Elasson** or **Minor** (:term:`Mizon & Elasson <mizon-elasson>`). From the third (rectangle expressible, sum *Meson*) arise *Potens effabile et meson* and *Faciens cum effabili meson totum*; and from the fourth (Definition XIX) *Potens bina media* and *Faciens cum meso meson totum*. Behold the true origin and cause of all **twelve Euclidean species** of Book X!
