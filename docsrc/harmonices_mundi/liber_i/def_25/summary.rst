Definition XXV: Summary
=======================

Culminates Kepler's taxonomic reconstruction of Euclid Book X, yielding **Mizon** (pentagon chord/diagonal :math:`d_5 = \frac{R}{2}\sqrt{10+2\sqrt{5}}`) and **Elasson** (pentagon side :math:`s_5 = \frac{R}{2}\sqrt{10-2\sqrt{5}}`).
