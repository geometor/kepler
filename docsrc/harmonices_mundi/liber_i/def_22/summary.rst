Definition XXII: Summary
========================

Directly names the **Apotome** (:math:`a - \sqrt{b}`) exemplified geometrically by the regular **Decagon** (:math:`s_{10} = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2} R`, Euclid XIII.9).
