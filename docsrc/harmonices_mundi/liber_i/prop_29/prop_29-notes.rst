Proposition XXIX: Notes & Provenance
====================================

Ptolemy's Construction & Euclid XIII.5
--------------------------------------

In *Almagest* I.10, :person:`Ptolemy <ptolemy>` derived his chord table using the construction Kepler formalizes in Proposition XXIX: bisecting radius $OB$ at $M$ and swinging arc $MA$ down to the horizontal diameter cuts off $OG = s_{10} = R/\phi$. Euclid demonstrates in *Elements* XIII.5 that adding the whole to the greater segment yields a new line cut in the same ratio, a property Kepler exalts as divine self-generation.
