Proposition XLV: Summary
========================

Kepler's Anticipation of Galois Multiplicity
--------------------------------------------

In Proposition XLV, :person:`Kepler <kepler>` points out not only that :person:`Jost Bürgi's <buergi>` chord polynomial $y^6 - 7y^4 + 14y^2 - 7 = 0$ has cubic degree in $y^2$, but also that its three positive roots $2\sin(\pi/7), 2\sin(2\pi/7), 2\sin(3\pi/7)$ are **algebraically indistinguishable conjugates** over $\mathbb{Q}$—remarkably foreshadowing Galois-group symmetry two centuries before Galois and :person:`Wantzel <wantzel>` (:cite:`1837 <wantzel:1837>`).
