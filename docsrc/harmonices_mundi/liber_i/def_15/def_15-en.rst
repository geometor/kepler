Definition XV: Fourth Degree (Medial / Mese)
============================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0027
   :page: p. 10

The degrees that follow are all called *ἄλογοι*, **inexpressible** (which Latin translators have rendered *Irrationales*—with great danger of ambiguity and absurdity, says Kepler, since such lines stand in the finest ratios; we therefore bury that word!). The **fourth degree** is the first of the inexpressibles, when neither the line nor its square is rational, yet its square transforms into a rectangle whose sides are expressible in square only—this line is called **Mese** (:term:`Mese <mese>`, mean proportional, Euclid X.21).
