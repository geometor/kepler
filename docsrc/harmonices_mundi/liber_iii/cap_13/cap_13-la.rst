Caput XIII — Quid sit Cantus Naturaliter Concinnus & Aptus
==========================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0151
   :page: pp. 61–65

.. rst-class:: latin-source

*Concinnus igitur & humanarum aurium judicio aptus cantus est, qui exorsus a certo quodam sono, ab eo per intervalla concinna tendit ad sonos consonos. Exemplum: Victimae paschali laudes immolent Christiani.*
