Caput VI — De Cantus Generibus, Duro & Molli
============================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0133
   :page: p. 43

.. rst-class:: latin-source

*De cantus generibus, Duro & Molli.* Hinc ergo nascuntur duo sectionum Genera: Genus molle ($12, 15, 16, 18, 20, 24$) & Genus durum ($30, 36, 40, 45, 48, 60$), cum suis medietatibus in notis.
