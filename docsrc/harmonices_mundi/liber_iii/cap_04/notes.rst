Chapter IV: Notes & Provenance
==============================

Spans folios ``0121``–``0129`` (pp. 31–39) of the 1619 Linz *editio princeps*.

Apparatus & Diagrams
--------------------

* **Musical Notation (*In notis, ex anticipato*)**: Folios ``0125`` (p. 35), ``0126`` (p. 36), ``0128`` (p. 38), and ``0129`` (p. 39) include early mensural staff examples anticipating the notation system formalized in Chapter IX, alongside the monochord division of length :math:`120` into :math:`60, 72, 75, 80, 90, 96, 100, 120`.
* **Arithmetical Corollary (p. 37)**: Pairs :math:`n^2` against :math:`(n-1)(n+1)` for :math:`n = 2 \dots 9`, noting that :math:`7^2 = 49` with :math:`35, 63` and :math:`10^2 = 100` with :math:`9 \cdot 11 = 99` fail because :math:`7, 9, 11` belong to non-constructible regular polygons.
* **Terminological Note (p. 34, 38)**: On p. 34 (*ut 1. 9. est quidem tripla ipsius 1. 3.*), Kepler writes *tripla* where mathematically :math:`1:9 = (1:3)^2` is the duplicate ratio of :math:`1:3`. On p. 38, he warns against earlier theorists who called :math:`15:16` the *minor tone*.
