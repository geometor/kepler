Chapter IV: Summary
===================

Derives the exact 5-limit step ratios (:math:`8:9, 9:10, 15:16, 24:25`) from subtracting consonant ratios rather than composing from a minimal unit, defines third-order intervals (:math:`80:81` syntonic comma, :math:`128:135` irregular diesis/limma, :math:`225:256`, :math:`243:256` Platonic limma, :math:`2048:2187` apotome), presents the arithmetical square/oblong corollary for :math:`1`–:math:`10`, and explains why pitch height (*acutum/grave*, *altum/profundum*) yields geometric intervals.
