Chapter XII: Summary
====================

Exposes the six **comma-impure ("wolf") consonances** (*consonantiae adulterinae*) that arise across the octave junction of the Just-Intonation *Disdiapason*: the Pythagorean ditone :math:`64:81`, abundant fourth :math:`20:27`, deficient fourth :math:`243:320`, deficient minor sixth :math:`81:128`, deficient fifth :math:`27:40` (:math:`D`–:math:`a` / :math:`a`–:math:`ee` / :math:`bb`–:math:`ff`), and abundant fifth :math:`160:243`, each differing from a pure consonance by one **Syntonic Comma** (:math:`80:81`).
