Propositions XXV–XXVIII: Executive Geometric Summary
====================================================

Completeness of the 13 Archimedean Convex Solids
------------------------------------------------

By separating prisms ($4.4.n$) and antiprisms ($3.3.3.n$) into infinite families, :person:`Kepler <kepler>` gave the first complete modern proof that exactly **13** convex vertex-transitive semi-regular solids exist, alongside the two regular non-convex Kepler-Poinsot star polyhedra $\{5/2, 5\}$ and $\{5/2, 3\}$.
