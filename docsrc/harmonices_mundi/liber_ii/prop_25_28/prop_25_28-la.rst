Propositiones XXV–XXVIII — Corpora Stellata, Rhombica & Archimedea
==================================================================

*Public-domain Neo-Latin text from Ioannis Keppleri Harmonices Mundi Liber II, Propositiones XXV–XXVIII* (:cite:`Linz, 1619, pp. 62–66 <kepler:1619:hm>`; :cite:`ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. V, pp. 140–144 <frisch:1864:v5>`).

.. rst-class:: latin-source

In solido denique demonstrantur:

1. **Quinque Corpora Regularia Primaria** :person:`Platonis <plato>` et :person:`Euclidis <euclid>`.
2. **Duo Corpora Regularia Stellata** (augmentata seu echinata): ex duodecim :term:`stellis quinquangulis <stella-quinquangula>` $\{5/2\}$ invicem connexis nascuntur duo corpora perfectissime regularia—alterum duodecim cuspidum quinariarum (**Dodecaedron Stellatum Minus**, $\{5/2, 5\}$), alterum viginti cuspidum ternariarum (**Dodecaedron Stellatum Maius**, $\{5/2, 3\}$).
3. **Duo Corpora ex Rhombis Aequalibus**: :term:`Dodecaedron Rhombicum <dodecaedron-rhombicum>` ($\sqrt{2}:1$) et :term:`Triacontaedron Rhombicum <triacontaedron-rhombicum>` (:g:`PQRS`, cuius diagonales :g:`PR_gold` et :g:`QS_gold` tenent :term:`proportionem divinam <sectio-divina>` $\phi : 1$).
4. **Tredecim Corpora Semiregularia :person:`Archimedis <archimedes>`** (Prop. XXVIII, seclusis infinitis columnis prismaticis et antiprismaticis): inter quae eminent *Cuboctaedron*, *Icosidodecaedron*, ac duo corpora chirali obliquitate insignia, **Cubus simus** et **Dodecaedron simum** (:cite:`Pappus, Synagoge V <pappus:synagoge:v>`).
