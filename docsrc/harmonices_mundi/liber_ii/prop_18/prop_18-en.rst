Proposition XVIII: Only Three Pure Regular Planar Tilings
=========================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0069
   :page: p. 51

Pure plane tiling from a **single species** of regular polygon is possible in **three and only three ways**: six equilateral triangles (`3.3.3.3.3.3`), four squares (`4.4.4.4`), or three regular hexagons (`6.6.6`). Three pentagons leave a gap ($324^\circ < 360^\circ$), four overflow ($432^\circ > 360^\circ$), and all polygons with $n \ge 7$ exceed $360^\circ$ already at three faces. Kepler also notes equilateral triangular rhombi (*G*) and hexagram star-extensions (*K*).
