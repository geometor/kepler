Proposition XIV: At Least Three Angles Meet in Plane Congruence
===============================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0068
   :page: p. 50

No fewer than three plane angles can form a planar congruence around a point. Since the full circumference around any vertex equals four right angles ($360^\circ$) and every convex polygon angle is strictly less than two right angles ($< 180^\circ$), two angles alone always sum to less than four right angles and cannot fill the plane.
