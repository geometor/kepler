Proposition XVI: Angle-Sum Threshold (4 Right Angles Plane vs. Solid)
=====================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0068
   :page: pp. 50–51

The sum of plane angles converging in planar congruence is **always equal to four right angles** ($360^\circ$), never greater; whereas in solid congruence the sum is **strictly less than four right angles** ($< 360^\circ$). Drawing together the two edges bordering a gap $< 360^\circ$ closes the hiatus and forces the vertex upward into three dimensions.
