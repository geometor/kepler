Proposition XXI: Plane Figures of Four or More Species Do Not Meet
==================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0073
   :page: p. 57

**Proposition XXI.**

*Plane figures of four or more species are not congruent by single angles to fill a solid place.*

For the four smallest angles are these: the triangular, the square, the pentagonal, and the hexagonal. Yet the first and last of these equal two right angles, the second is one right angle, and the third exceeds a right angle by one fifth part of a right angle. Joined together, therefore, they are greater than four right angles; wherefore by Proposition XVI they are not congruent. Much more, therefore, will four right angles be exceeded by larger angles.
