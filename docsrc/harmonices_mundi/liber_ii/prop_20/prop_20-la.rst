Propositio XX — Quatuor Casus Trium Specierum & Plates Cc–Nn
============================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0071
   :page: pp. 55–56

.. rst-class:: latin-source

*Ex planis angulis trium specierum, locus planus congrue impletur quater.* Trigonus unus, tetragonus unus, hexagonus? Aut duo trigonici cum tetragonico & dodecagonico; tetragonicus cum hexagonico & dodecagonico.
