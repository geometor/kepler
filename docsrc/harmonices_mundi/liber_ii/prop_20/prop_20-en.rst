Proposition XX: Three-Species Planar Tilings (Plates Cc–Nn & `4.6.12`, `3.4.6.4`)
=================================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0071
   :page: pp. 55–56

When **three species** of regular polygons meet around a point, planar congruence succeeds across **four arithmetic cases**, yielding the remaining Archimedean tilings—`4.6.12` (square, hexagon, dodecagon, *Mm*) and `3.4.6.4` (triangle, two squares, hexagon, *Ii* continuable vs. *Kk* non-continuable)—while excluding non-extendable clusters such as triangle-square-dodecagon (*Cc–Ee*) and square-pentagon-icosagon (*Ll*):
