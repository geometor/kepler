Proposition XXVI: Summary
=========================

Discovery of the Two Regular Non-Convex Star Polyhedra
------------------------------------------------------

Extending regular solid congruence to star-pentagram faces $\{5/2\}$, Kepler discovers the first two regular non-convex polyhedra (later completed to four Kepler–Poinsot solids by Louis Poinsot in 1809):

1. **Small Stellated Dodecahedron** ($\{5/2, 5\}$, Plate ``Ss``): 12 pentagrammic faces, 12 five-pointed vertices, 30 edges (Euler characteristic $\chi = -6$).
2. **Great Stellated Dodecahedron** ($\{5/2, 3\}$, Plate ``Tt``): 12 pentagrammic faces, 20 three-pointed vertices, 30 edges ($\chi = 2$).
