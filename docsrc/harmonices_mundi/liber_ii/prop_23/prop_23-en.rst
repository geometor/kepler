Proposition XXIII: Two Angles of an Odd Polygon Cannot Form a Regular Solid
===========================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0073
   :page: p. 57

Two plane angles belonging to an odd-sided polygon together with one angle of another species cannot form any uniform regular solid; for by Proposition XVII the solid corners would necessarily become heterogeneous around the odd-sided face.
