Proposition XVII: Odd-Sided Figures with Two Neighboring Species
================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0069
   :page: p. 51

**Proposition XVII.**

*An odd-sided figure to whose sides are fitted figures of two species cannot meet in the same form at all its angles, either in a plane or in a solid.*

For it comes about at one of its angles that figures of the same species stand on both sides, which does not happen at the other angles. For the reason of this, see figure *C* of the following copperplate.
