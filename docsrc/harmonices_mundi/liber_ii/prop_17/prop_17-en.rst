Proposition XVII: Alternating Parity Obstruction Around Odd Polygons
====================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0069
   :page: p. 51

An **odd-sided regular polygon** ($n$ odd—triangle, pentagon, heptagon) whose edges meet figures of two other distinct species cannot convene in uniform form around every vertex either in the plane or in a solid. For two distinct neighboring species cannot alternate evenly around an odd cycle without colliding identically at the closing edge.
