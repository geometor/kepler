Axiom XXII: Triangle Inequality for Three Plane Angles at a Solid Corner
========================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0073
   :page: p. 57

When two plane angles summed together are not greater than the third plane angle, they cannot enclose a solid angle with it (equivalent to Euclid XI.20).
