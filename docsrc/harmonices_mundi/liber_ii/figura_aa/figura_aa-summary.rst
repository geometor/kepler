Figura Aa: Executive Geometric Summary
======================================

Kepler–Poinsot Star Polyhedra & Non-Periodic Tiling Precursor
-------------------------------------------------------------

Book II introduces the **Small Stellated Dodecahedron** $\{5/2, 5\}$ and **Great Stellated Dodecahedron** $\{5/2, 3\}$ (:cite:`Poinsot 1810 <poinsot:1810>`) alongside the **Figure Aa** pentagram–decagon tiling that directly inspired :person:`Roger Penrose's <penrose>` aperiodic P1 tiling (:cite:`1974 <penrose:1974>`).
