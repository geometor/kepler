Proposition XXVIII: Notes & Provenance
======================================

Transcription & Provenance
--------------------------

* **Primary Witness**: Johannes Kepler, *Harmonices Mundi Libri V*, Liber II, Propositio XXVIII (:cite:`Linz: Tampach/Plancus, 1619, pp. 61–66 <kepler:1619:hm>`; :cite:`ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. V, pp. 141–144 <frisch:1864:v5>`).
* **Folio Scans**: Verified against 300-DPI scans ``harmonices_mundi:0085`` (p. 61, enunciation) and ``harmonices_mundi:0086`` (p. 62, engraved polyhedra ``8``–``13``).
* **English Track**: Original GEOMETOR English translation.

Kepler's Enduring Polyhedral Nomenclature
-----------------------------------------

Every modern student of geometry speaks Kepler's Latin daily: *cuboctahedron*, *icosidodecahedron*, *rhombic dodecahedron*, *rhombic triacontahedron*, *truncation*, and *snub* (*simus*—"snub-nosed", for the chiral `3.3.3.3.4` and `3.3.3.3.5` solids) were all coined or standardized in *Harmonices Mundi* Book II (:cite:`Coxeter 1973 <coxeter:1973>`).
