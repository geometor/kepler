Sphere Close-Packing: Summary
=============================

The Kepler Sphere-Packing Conjecture
------------------------------------

Distinguishing simple cubic packing (kissing number $6$, density $\frac{\pi}{6} \approx 0.52360$) from face-centered cubic / hexagonal pyramidal close-packing (kissing number $12$, tetrahedral layer height $\sqrt{2/3}\,d$), :person:`Kepler <kepler>` asserted maximal packing density:

$$\eta_{\text{FCC/HCP}} = \frac{\pi}{3\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{18}} \approx 0.74048, \qquad \frac{\eta_{\text{FCC/HCP}}}{\eta_{\text{cubic}}} = \sqrt{2},$$

and showed that uniform isotropic compression transforms close-packed spheres into space-filling **Rhombic Dodecahedra** (the exact Voronoi cell of the $A_3 \cong D_3$ FCC lattice).
