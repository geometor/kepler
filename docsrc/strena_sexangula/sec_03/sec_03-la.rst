Sectio III: De Malo Granato & Coaptatione Globulorum Arctissima
===============================================================

.. rst-class:: latin-source

Transi iam ad **malum granatum**, in quo cernimus acinos figura rhombica angulatos. Quae causa huius figurae? Non profecto anima formalis angulos numerans, sed **ipsa materiae necessitas et mutua globulorum compressio**!

Nam si errantes in eodem plano horizontali globulos aequales coegeris in angustum, ut se mutuo contingant, aut **quadrangulari** forma (A) coeunt, aut **triangulari** (B); ibi quatuor unum circumstant, **hic sex unum circumstant**.

   .. picture:: 0031.g1.png
      :alt: Ordo quadrangularis A et triangularis B (Francofurti 1611, p. 9)
      :caption: Editio princeps (Francofurti 1611, p. 9, fol. 0031): Ordines plani A et B.

   .. picture:: 0031.g1.svg
      :alt: Schema vectoriale ordinum A et B
      :caption: Schema vectoriale: Ordo quadrangularis (A) et ordo triangularis-hexagonus (B).

Iam superstruantur ordines alii in solido:

* Si quadrangularis ordo directe ordini quadrangulari imponatur, singuli globuli a **senis** tantum contingentur ($\pi / 6$).
* At si in cavo quatuor globulorum ordinis quadrangularis—aut in cavo trium globulorum ordinis triangularis (strata A–E, p. 10)—globulus superioris strati locetur, tunc quilibet globulus interior **in universum a duodecim tangetur** (sex in eodem plano, tribus supra, tribus infra)!

   .. picture:: 0032.g1.png

   .. picture:: 0032.g1.svg

   *"**Coaptatio fiet arctissima, ut nullo praeterea ordine plures globuli in idem vas compingi queant.** Rursum igitur hisce globulis aequaliter intumescentibus aut undique compressis, fient ex globosis **rhombica duodecim facierum**!"* (:term:`Coniectura Kepleri <kepler-conjecture>`, $\frac{\pi}{3\sqrt{2}}$).
