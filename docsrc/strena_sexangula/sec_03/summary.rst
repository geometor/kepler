Sphere Close-Packing: Summary
=============================

The Kepler Sphere-Packing Conjecture
------------------------------------

Distinguishing simple cubic packing (kissing number :math:`6`, density :math:`\frac{\pi}{6} \approx 0.52360`) from face-centered cubic / hexagonal pyramidal close-packing (kissing number :math:`12`, tetrahedral layer height :math:`\sqrt{2/3}\,d`), :person:`Kepler <kepler>` asserted maximal packing density:

.. math::

   \eta_{\text{FCC/HCP}} = \frac{\pi}{3\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{18}} \approx 0.74048, \qquad \frac{\eta_{\text{FCC/HCP}}}{\eta_{\text{cubic}}} = \sqrt{2},

and showed that uniform isotropic compression transforms close-packed spheres into space-filling **Rhombic Dodecahedra** (the exact Voronoi cell of the :math:`A_3 \cong D_3` FCC lattice).
