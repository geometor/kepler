Dedication & Snow Stars: Summary
================================

Birth of Mathematical Crystallography
-------------------------------------

Prompted by six-cornered snow crystals falling on his coat on the Prague bridge, :person:`Kepler <kepler>` formulated the foundational principle of structural crystallography—macroscopic symmetry reflects discrete geometric packing of microscopic elementary globules.
