Pentamerous Blossoms & Fibonacci: Notes & Provenance
====================================================

Fibonacci Recurrence and Morphogenetic Teleology
------------------------------------------------

In §§15–19, Kepler is the first mathematician to formulate the integer recurrence $1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21\dots$ explicitly as an algebraic approximation to the continuous ratio of Euclid Book II.11 and VI.30. He identifies the vegetative *anima formatrix* as an operating algorithm that steps discrete integers forward through successive additions ($F_{n+1} = F_n + F_{n-1}$) to realize golden pentagonal geometry in flowers and pinecones without requiring radical calculations.
