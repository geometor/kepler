Definition I: Notes & Provenance¶

Printed directly below the ornamental tailpiece and heading De Figurarum Regularium demonstrationibus on p. 6 alongside the woodcut diagram of square $QPRO$ enclosing cross $MLN$.

Transcription & Provenance¶

  • Latin Witness: Public-domain Neo-Latin text from Ioannis Keppleri Harmonices Mundi (Linz: Tampach/Plancus, 1619, p. 6; ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. V, pp. 78–92).

  • English Track: Original GEOMETOR English translation & critical apparatus (Linz, 1619, p. 6).

  • Folio Scan: Verified 300-DPI primary scan harmonices_mundi:0023 (p. 6).
