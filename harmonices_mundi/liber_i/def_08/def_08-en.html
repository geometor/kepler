Definition VIII: Knowable (Scibile)¶

Knowable (scibile) is said of that which is either immediately measurable through itself by the diameter, if it is a line, or by its square, if it is a surface; or which is formed at least from such quantities by a definite and geometrical ratio, which—however long the series—nevertheless depends at last upon the Diameter or its square. In Greek it is called γνώριμον (gnorimon).
