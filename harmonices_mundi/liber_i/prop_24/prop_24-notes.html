Proposition XXIV: Notes & Provenance¶

Spans folio 0031 (p. 13) bottom onto 0032 (p. 14) with the gnomon-subdivided square diagram labeled $\theta, \lambda, \pi, \sigma, \mu, \kappa, \xi, o$.

Textual & Mathematical Notes¶

  • Modification of Euclid X.33: Where Euclid X.33 constructs two lines incommensurable in square whose sum of squares is medial and rectangle expressible, Kepler adapts the proof to the fifth degree (Definition XVII) by replacing lines expressible in power only (ῥηταῖς δυνάμει μόνον) with lines expressible in length (ῥηταὶ μήκει) and replacing medial (μέσον) with expressible (ῥητόν).

  • 1619 Print Orthography: In folio 0032 line 10, the Linz print reads ejus quandum κο (a compositor’s slip for quadrum, as printed two words later in ejus quadrum θκ).

Transcription & Provenance¶

  • Latin Witness: Public-domain Neo-Latin text from Ioannis Keppleri Harmonices Mundi (Linz: Tampach/Plancus, 1619, pp. 13–14; ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. V, pp. 78–92).

  • English Track: Original GEOMETOR English translation & critical apparatus (Linz, 1619, pp. 13–14).

  • Folio Scan: Verified 300-DPI primary scans harmonices_mundi:0031 (p. 13) and harmonices_mundi:0032 (p. 14).
