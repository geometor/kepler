Prooemium: Executive Geometric Summary¶

Purpose of Euclid Book X Recovered¶

Against Petrus Ramus, Kepler identifies the architectural telos of Euclid’s classification of quadratic irrationals: establishing a rigorous hierarchy of degrees of geometric knowability (scibilitas) that separates constructible regular polygons from non-constructible ones.
