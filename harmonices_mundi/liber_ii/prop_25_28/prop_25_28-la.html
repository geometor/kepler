Propositiones XXV–XXVIII — Corpora Stellata, Rhombica & Archimedea¶

Public-domain Neo-Latin text from Ioannis Keppleri Harmonices Mundi Liber II, Propositiones XXV–XXVIII (Linz, 1619, pp. 62–66; ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. V, pp. 140–144).

In solido denique demonstrantur:

  1. Quinque Corpora Regularia Primaria Platonis et Euclidis.

  2. Duo Corpora Regularia Stellata (augmentata seu echinata): ex duodecim stellis quinquangulis $\{5/2\}$ invicem connexis nascuntur duo corpora perfectissime regularia—alterum duodecim cuspidum quinariarum (Dodecaedron Stellatum Minus, $\{5/2, 5\}$), alterum viginti cuspidum ternariarum (Dodecaedron Stellatum Maius, $\{5/2, 3\}$).

  3. Duo Corpora ex Rhombis Aequalibus: Dodecaedron Rhombicum ($\sqrt{2}:1$) et Triacontaedron Rhombicum (PQRS, cuius diagonales PRgold et QSgold tenent proportionem divinam $\phi : 1$).

  4. Tredecim Corpora Semiregularia :person:`Archimedis <archimedes>` (Prop. XXVIII, seclusis infinitis columnis prismaticis et antiprismaticis): inter quae eminent Cuboctaedron, Icosidodecaedron, ac duo corpora chirali obliquitate insignia, Cubus simus et Dodecaedron simum (Pappus, Synagoge V).
