Chapter I: Essence of Sensible and Intellectual Harmonies

As I am about to discourse upon the essence of Harmonies, I find myself in doubt whether it is clearer first to examine the opinions of the ancients and then compare them with my own, or to begin with an exposition of my own view. The former way of philosophizing is received by all and frequently commended by Aristotle; the latter seems better suited to the present matter. For few have touched upon disputations concerning the Essence of Harmonies in particular; and whatever they have said about the subsistence of Mathematical Genera and species that could be applied to Harmonies in particular must necessarily be full of obscurity in the philosophy of the Gentiles, and thereby leave the reader’s mind wavering in doubt between the love of most plausible doctrines and the suspicion of an invented fiction. In Christian assemblies, however, and if a man embraces with firm faith the most sacred Mystery of the Trinity and the origin of all things from the Mosaic history, the heads of the disputation can both be set forth more clearly and find the minds of readers more inclined to belief. That we may therefore make clear the method of what is to be said, we must begin from a division. For sensible Harmony, or what is analogous to it, is one thing; Harmony separated and pure from sensible things is another. The former are many, both by reason of their subjects differing in species and by reason of individuals; whereas pure Harmony, separated from sensible subjects, is one and the same in any species whatsoever. For example, there is one and the same species of Harmony which arises from the double proportion: if this resides in sounds, it is called the Diapason (octave); if in radiations, it is named the Opposite Aspect; and in a Musical system it is either higher or lower, either sharper or deeper, either of human voices or of sounds from instruments; nor is it less manifold in Meteorology, for it belongs either to Saturn and Jupiter or to another pair, and either from the equinoctial signs or from the Tropics.
