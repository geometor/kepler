The Harmony of the World¶

Ioannis Keppleri Harmonices Mundi Libri V (Linz: Tampach/Plancus, 1619)

Johannes Kepler
Harmonices Mundi Libri V (The Harmony of the World in Five Books, 1619)

Geometric Scibilitas of Constructible Polygons, Architectonic Congruence of Planar Tilings and Polyhedra, and the Harmonic Third Law of Planetary Motion

Primary Latin Witness: Editio princeps (Linz: Tampach / excudebat Ioannes Plancus, 1619); Christian Frisch, Opera Omnia V (1864), pp. 75–334.
GEOMETOR Edition: English Translation, Executive Summaries, Commentary & Exact Symbolic Verifications across Books I, II, and V.

The Five Books of The Harmony of the World¶

Book I: On the Origin of Regular Figures

Book I of The Harmony of the World: On the Origin of Regular Harmonic Figures, recovering Euclid Book X, defining the eight degrees of geometric scibilitas, and demonstrating the non-constructibility of the regular heptagon.

Book II: On the Congruence of Figures

Book II of The Harmony of the World: Architectonic Congruence of Regular Figures, complete classification of the 11 uniform Archimedean planar tilings, Figure Aa star congruence, and the 13 Archimedean solids.

Book V: On the Harmony of Celestial Motions

Book V of The Harmony of the World & the 1597 Epistola: The Kepler Right Triangle (1, sqrt(phi), phi), planetary aphelion/perihelion consonances, and Kepler’s Harmonic Third Law ((T1/T2)^2 = (a1/a2)^3).
