The Six-Cornered Snowflake¶

Ioannis Kepleri S. Cæs. Maiest. Mathematici Strena seu de Nive Sexangula (Frankfurt am Main: Tampach, 1611)

Johannes Kepler

The Foundations of Mathematical Crystallography, Close-Packed Sphere Lattices, the Rhombic Dodecahedron, and Golden Botanical Phyllotaxis

Dedication & Snow Stars on the Prague Bridge

Since I am well aware, Illustrious Herr Wackher, how keenly you delight in Nothing (Nihil)—not for its bareness of price, to be sure, yet for the witty play of delightful sparrows—I readily conjecture that a New Year’s gift (Strena) will prove all the dearer to you the nearer it approaches to Nothing. Anxious in thought as to where I might find something virtually indistinguishable from Nothing, I happened just then to be crossing the bridge at Prague, mortified by my lack of a worthy gift, when by a timely chance thickened vapor condensed into snow, and scattered little flakes fell here and there upon my coat—every single one six-cornered, with feathered rays (omnes sexanguli, villosis radiis).

Bee Honeycombs & the Two Rhombic Polyhedra

Let us examine familiar natural architectures where regular geometry arises from material necessity. Bees build hexagonal wax prisms (Pappus, Synagoge V) so that every wall is shared by two neighboring chambers without empty interstices. Yet inspect the bottoms (fundi) of a two-layered honeycomb! Each six-sided tube terminates not in a flat floor, but in an obtuse three-plane pyramidal keel composed of three congruent rhombi, meeting three-by-three against the staggered cells of the opposite layer.

Pomegranate Seeds & Sphere Close-Packing

Turn next to the pomegranate (malum granatum), whose angular crimson kernels are packed tightly inside a tough rind. Why are those seeds faceted into rhomboidal planes? Not from any formal soul counting faces, declare I, but from material compression of round globules!

Pentamerous Blossoms & Fibonacci Convergence

Why then do snowflakes exhibit sixfold crystalline rays while the blossoms of apple, pear, rose, and cinquefoil unfold in fivefold petals (ABCDE), never sixfold or sevenfold? Because crystalline vapor condenses under cold spatial geometry ($60^\circ$ planar close-packing), whereas living plants are fashioned after an archetypal formative faculty grounded in Euclid’s self-propagating Divine Proportion!
