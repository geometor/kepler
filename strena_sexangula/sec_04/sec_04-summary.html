Pentamerous Blossoms & Fibonacci: Summary¶

Botanical Pentamery & Fibonacci Convergence to $\phi$¶

Contrasting inanimate sixfold ice crystals with living five-petaled blossoms, Kepler connected Euclid’s self-propagating Divine Section ($1 : \phi = \phi : (1 + \phi)$) to the integer recurrence $F_{n+1} = F_n + F_{n-1}$ ($1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21\dots$), observing both monotone rational convergence $F_{n+1}/F_n \to \phi$ and the alternating overshoot/undershoot captured by Cassini’s identity $F_{n+1}F_{n-1} - F_n^2 = (-1)^n$.
