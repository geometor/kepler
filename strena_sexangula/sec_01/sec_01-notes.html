Dedication & Snow Stars: Notes & Provenance¶

Literary Paradox (Nix / Nihil) and Morphological Method¶

Composed at Imperial Prague in late 1610 as a playful humanist gift for Privy Councillor J. M. Wackher von Wackenfels, Strena stands in the Renaissance tradition of paradoxical encomia (Praise of Nothing). Yet within twenty-four quarto pages Kepler inaugurated three modern disciplines simultaneously:

  • Geometric Crystallography & Lattice Theory (later developed by Steno, Hooke, Huygens, Haüy, and Bravais);

  • Discrete & Computational Geometry (sphere packing, kissing numbers, and 3D Voronoi/Dirichlet tessellations, Thompson 1942);

  • Mathematical Phyllotaxis (linking Fibonacci parastichy numbers to the golden section, developed across geometor/phyllotaxis).
