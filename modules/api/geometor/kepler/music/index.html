geometor.kepler.music¶

Exact Symbolic Just-Intonation Music Engine for Kepler’s Harmonices Mundi (1619).

Transcribes verbatim from Kepler’s Linz 1619 folios (0297, 0299, 0301, 0302) and Mysterium Cosmographicum (1596, folio 0050):

  • All 7 engraved planetary melodies on folio 0299 (p. 207): Saturnus, Jupiter, Mars, Terra (MI–FA–MI), Venus, Mercurius, and Luna (Hic locum habet etiam ☽).

  • All 4 Universal 6-Planet Creation Chords on folios 0301–0302 (pp. 209–210): Genus Durum (ut ♮, ut c) and Genus Molle (ut b, ut c).

  • Exact integer string-length scales from folio 0297 (2160 down to 864).

  • Multi-track Standard MIDI File (Format 1 with 14-bit Just-Intonation pitch bend) and stereo Cathedral Organ / Celestial Choir WAV synthesizer.
