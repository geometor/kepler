geometor.kepler.strena_packing¶

Exact symbolic geometry of Kepler’s Strena seu de Nive Sexangula (1611).

Provides 100% exact SymPy 3D analyses of Kepler’s two isohedral rhombic polyhedra—the space-filling Rhombic Dodecahedron (diagonal ratio sqrt(2):1, dihedral 120 deg) and the golden Rhombic Triacontahedron (diagonal ratio phi:1, dihedral 144 deg)—verifies the cubic/hexagonal sphere close-packing density pi / (3*sqrt(2)) against simple cubic pi / 6, constructs the 7-circle hexagonal close-packing rosette in geometor.model, and computes exact rational Fibonacci phyllotaxis convergents to phi.

Construct Kepler’s planar 7-circle hexagonal close-packing / snowflake rosette.
