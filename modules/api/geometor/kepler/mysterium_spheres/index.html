geometor.kepler.mysterium_spheres¶

Exact symbolic geometry of Kepler’s Mysterium Cosmographicum (1596 / 1621).

Derives the insphere-to-circumsphere radius and quadrance ratios of the five regular Platonic solids directly from exact 3D vertex coordinates over Q(phi), proves polar-duality ratio equality (Cube = Octahedron, Dodecahedron = Icosahedron), computes the heliocentric nested planetary sphere radii from Saturn to Mercury with Earth as Circulus mensor omnium, and constructs Kepler’s July 19, 1595 Graz Great-Conjunction planar nesting in geometor.model with zero floating-point approximations.

geometor.kepler.mysterium_spheres.compute_nested_planetary_radii(earth_radius: sympy.Expr | int = 1) → tuple[NestedPlanetOrb, ...]¶

Compute exact symbolic radii from Saturn to Mercury (Mysterium Cap. XIV).

Takes the Telluric orb (Terra est Circulus mensor omnium) as earth_radius and propagates outward via circumscribed Dodecahedron, Tetrahedron, and Cube, and inward via inscribed Icosahedron and Octahedron.

geometor.kepler.mysterium_spheres.construct_mysterium_planar_nesting(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → geometor.model.Model¶

Construct Kepler’s July 19, 1595 Graz Great-Conjunction planar diagram.

Starting purely from rational points O = (0, 0) and A = (0, 1), constructs the circumscribed circle c_out, the inscribed equilateral triangle ABC with incircle c_in_tri (Q_in / Q_out = 1/4, r_in / R_out = 1/2), and the inscribed square AESW with incircle c_in_sq (Q_in / Q_out = 1/2).
