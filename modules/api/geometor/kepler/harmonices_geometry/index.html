geometor.kepler.harmonices_geometry¶

Exact symbolic geometry of Kepler’s Harmonices Mundi (1619) & 1597 Kepler Triangle.

Implements:

  1. Pure straightedge-and-compass geometor.model construction & Rational Trigonometry proof of the Kepler Right Triangle (1, sqrt(phi), phi) over Q(sqrt(phi)) (Epistola ad Mästlinum, Oct 1597).

  2. Exact cyclotomic minimal-polynomial classifier separating constructible polygons (Scibilia) from the non-constructible Regular Heptagon x^3 + x^2 - 2*x - 1 = 0 and Bürgi’s cossic chord equation (Book I).

  3. Exact Diophantine + global edge-consistency solver deriving all 11 uniform Archimedean planar tilings and verifying Kepler’s pentagonal/decagonal star congruence Figure Aa (Book II).

  4. Exact rational verification of Kepler’s Third Law (T1/T2)^2 = (a1/a2)^3 and planetary aphelion/perihelion musical consonances (Book V).

geometor.kepler.harmonices_geometry.construct_kepler_right_triangle(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → geometor.model.Model¶

Construct the Kepler Right Triangle (1, sqrt(phi), phi) purely from (0,0) & (1,0).

Uses geometor.model straightedge-and-compass intersections (auto_intersect=False):

  1. Constructs W = (-1, 0), perpendicular axis ON through O = (0, 0) with N = (0, 1), and midpoint E = (1/2, 0) of OA.

  2. Circle centered at E through N cuts OA extended at P = (phi, 0) (II.11).

  3. Midpoint K = ((phi - 1)/2, 0) of WP centers the Thales circle through P and W = (-1, 0), cutting the positive y-axis at B = (0, sqrt(phi)) (VI.13).

  4. Triangle OAB has exact quadrances Q(O,A) = 1, Q(O,B) = phi, Q(A,B) = 1 + phi = phi^2, and altitude OH cuts AB into BH = 1 and HA = 1/phi = phi - 1.
