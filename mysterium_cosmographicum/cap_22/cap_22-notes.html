Chapter XXII: Notes & Provenance¶

The Direct Seed of Kepler’s Second Law (Equal Areas)¶

Folio 0090 (p. 78) is arguably the single most prophetic page in the entire Mysterium Cosmographicum. Notice three historic milestones converging on one page: (1) Kepler praises Tycho Brahe by name for dissolving solid celestial spheres; (2) he replaces kinematic equants/epicycles with a physical radial force law centered at the true Sun $A$ (inverse-distance speed law $v \propto 1/r$, which is exact at aphelion and perihelion for an ellipse under conservation of angular momentum!); and (3) he flags Earth’s missing equant as an unresolved anomaly—the very clue that guided his war on Mars in Astronomia Nova (1609).

In the 1596 woodcut on folio 0090 (p. 78), the three circles are labeled IKPQ (outer, with R and S at the sides), EFGH (middle), and NOLM (inner); on p. 79 (folio 0091), Kepler occasionally refers to the inner and outer circles as NOPQ and IKLM in the prose—both letterings are preserved verbatim in the Latin and English tracks while linked to the calibrated vector diagram 0090.g1.svg.

Transcription & Provenance¶

  • Primary Witness: Johannes Kepler, Prodromus Dissertationum Cosmographicarum, continens Mysterium Cosmographicum (Tübingen, 1596, Caput XXII, pp. 77–80 (folios 0089–0092; equant/solar-force diagram on 0090); Editio altera cum notis authoris, Frankfurt, 1621; ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. I, pp. 171–174).

  • Folio Scan: Verified 300-DPI scans mysterium_cosmographicum:0089 (p. 77), 0090 (p. 78, Solar Motive Force & Equant Diagram), 0091 (p. 79), and 0092 (p. 80).
