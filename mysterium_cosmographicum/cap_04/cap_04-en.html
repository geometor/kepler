Chapter IV: Why Three Solids Surround Earth and Two Lie Within¶

Why do three bodies surround the Earth, while the remaining two clothe it within?

Allow me now, gentle Reader, to play for a little while in a serious matter, and to indulge somewhat in Allegories. For I think that very many causes of things in the world can be deduced from God’s love toward man. Certainly no one will deny that in adorning the dwelling-place of the world God looked again and again to its future inhabitant. For Man is the end both of the world and of all Creation. Therefore I think that the Earth, which was to bear and nourish the genuine image of the Creator, was deemed worthy by God to revolve among the middle planets in such a way that it would have just as many within the embrace of its orb as it would have outside it.

To obtain this, God counted the Sun among the remaining five Stars, even though it differed from them in its whole genus. And this seems all the more fitting because, since the Sun above was the image of God the Father, it is credible that by this association with the remaining Stars it was meant to furnish to the coming settler proofs of philanthropia and anthropopatheia, which God was to exercise toward human beings, stooping even to household familiarity. For in the Old Testament He frequently entered into the number of men, and willed to be called Abraham’s friend: just as we see the Sun enter into the number of moving bodies. Now since the Sun was encircled by the Earth, on the suppositions just stated, that order of solids necessarily had to be enclosed within the Earth which em-

braces at least two: namely, so that the two moving bodies together with the immobile Sun might make up the same ternary number that stands among those excluded from the orb of the Earth. Thus, then, with the Moon especially circling about the Earth, the supreme Creator placed our dwelling-place in the midst of seven Planets. For if the order of the remaining three had been added to the Sun, there would have been four Stars inside the Earth with the Sun, and only two outside; and since such a disorder (ataxia) of number lacks reason, it was passed over by the Creator. Likewise, since to contain belongs to the more perfect, as action, and to be contained to the less perfect, as passion, and the primary bodies are more perfect than the rest, it was fitting that the order of three should contain the Earth, while the remaining ones should be contained within the circuit of the terrestrial orb.

And thus we have in passing a reason why three Planets move outside the Earth, and two within: which if it does not fully satisfy the Reader, let him reflect that this is a bonus gift (honorarium), not our chief argument. For even if we did not know the reason why three Stars should go above the Earth (or above Ptolemy’s Sun), still what follows would stand firm with what went before, because we are certain of the FACT. Nor has anyone ever doubted that ♄, ♃, and ♂ are superior. Only let us hold fast to this: since in Copernicus three Planets are above the Earth, we must place the order of the three primary solids—Cube, Pyramid, Dodecahedron—outside the orb of the Earth, and the Octahedron and Icosahedron within, if we wish to win the palm in this undertaking.
