Unit II · Executive Geometric Summary¶

Core Discoveries of Strena seu de Nive Sexangula (1611)¶

  1. Birth of Mathematical Crystallography: Prompted by six-cornered snow crystals falling on his coat on the Prague bridge, Kepler formulated the foundational principle of structural crystallography—macroscopic symmetry reflects discrete geometric packing of microscopic elementary globules.

The Kepler Sphere-Packing Conjecture: Distinguishing simple cubic packing (kissing number $6$, density $\frac{\pi}{6} \approx 0.52360$) from face-centered cubic / hexagonal pyramidal close-packing (kissing number $12$, tetrahedral layer height $\sqrt{2/3}\,d$), Kepler asserted maximal packing density:

    $$\eta_{\text{FCC/HCP}} = \frac{\pi}{3\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{18}} \approx 0.74048, \qquad \frac{\eta_{\text{FCC/HCP}}}{\eta_{\text{cubic}}} = \sqrt{2},$$

    and showed that uniform isotropic compression transforms close-packed spheres into space-filling Rhombic Dodecahedra (the exact Voronoi cell of the $A_3 \cong D_3$ FCC lattice).

  2. Botanical Pentamery & Fibonacci Convergence to $\phi$: Contrasting inanimate sixfold ice crystals with living five-petaled blossoms, Kepler connected Euclid’s self-propagating Divine Section ($1 : \phi = \phi : (1 + \phi)$) to the integer recurrence $F_{n+1} = F_n + F_{n-1}$ ($1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21\dots$), observing both monotone rational convergence $F_{n+1}/F_n \to \phi$ and the alternating overshoot/undershoot captured by Cassini’s identity $F_{n+1}F_{n-1} - F_n^2 = (-1)^n$.
