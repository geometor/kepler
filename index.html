Johannes Kepler¶

The works of Johannes Kepler (1571–1630) revolutionized modern astronomy and geometry, uniting Copernican cosmology with the Platonic solids, founding mathematical crystallography, and discovering the harmonic laws of planetary motion. Within the interconnected GEOMETOR semantic digital archive, the Mysterium Cosmographicum, Strena seu de Nive Sexangula, and Harmonices Mundi are liberated from static print bounds—decomposed into addressable semantic units, calibrated vector diagrams, primary-source folio provenance, and cross-corpus citations speaking one shared geometric language.

About

geometor-kepler is a bilingual Digital Scriptorium Book Codex, semantic knowledge graph, and exact symbolic modeling environment dedicated to the foundational geometric and cosmological works of Johannes Kepler (1571–1630). Spanning three seminal treatises—Mysterium Cosmographicum (1596 / 1621), Strena seu de Nive Sexangula (On the Six-Cornered Snowflake, 1611), and Harmonices Mundi Libri V (The Harmony of the World, 1619)—this edition recovers Kepler’s revolutionary vision of geometry as the eternal archetypal blueprint of the physical universe.
