Johannes Kepler¶

Johannes Kepler (1571–1630) united geometry, music, and astronomy into a single search for the architecture of the cosmos—nesting the five Platonic solids between Copernican planetary orbs in 1596, asking why winter snowflakes always crystallize with six-fold symmetry in 1611, and unveiling the Third Law of planetary motion alongside the polyphonic Music of the Spheres in 1619. This edition presents all three Latin treatises in synchronized four-track Book Codices (Latin · English · Summary · Notes) paired with 616 archival folio scans, interactive 3D CAD reconstructions, and a live Just-Intonation celestial music studio.
