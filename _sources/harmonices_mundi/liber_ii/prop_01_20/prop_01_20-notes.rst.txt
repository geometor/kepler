Propositions I–XX: Historical & Mathematical Commentary
=======================================================

Classification History & The Enantiomorphic Snub
-------------------------------------------------

Kepler was the first mathematician in history to systematically enumerate and prove the completeness of the eleven uniform planar tilings. In particular, he recognized the chiral nature of the snub hexagonal tiling `3.3.3.3.6`, which exists in non-superimposable left- and right-handed mirror forms ($p6$), anticipating nineteenth-century crystallographic space-group theory.
