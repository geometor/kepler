Definitions: Executive Geometric Summary
========================================

Planar & Solid Congruence Criteria
----------------------------------

*Harmonices Mundi* Book II defines the conditions under which regular polygons meet edge-to-edge:

* **Planar Congruence**: Angle sum around each vertex must equal precisely $360^\circ$, with consistent global extension across all faces without self-contradiction;
* **Solid Congruence**: Angle sum around each vertex must be strictly less than $360^\circ$, folding into a closed polyhedron.
