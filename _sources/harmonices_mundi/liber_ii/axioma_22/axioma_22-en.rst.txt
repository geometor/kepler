Axiom XXII: On Two Plane Angles Not Greater Than a Third
========================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0073
   :page: p. 57

**Axiom XXII.**

*When two plane angles are not greater than the third, they do not form a solid angle with it.*
