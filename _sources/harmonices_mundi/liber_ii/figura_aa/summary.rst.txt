Figure Aa: Summary
==================

Kepler–Poinsot Star Polyhedra & Non-Periodic Tiling Precursor
-------------------------------------------------------------

Book II introduces the **Small Stellated Dodecahedron** :math:`\{5/2, 5\}` and **Great Stellated Dodecahedron** :math:`\{5/2, 3\}` (:cite:`Poinsot 1810 <poinsot:1810>`) alongside the **Figure Aa** pentagram–decagon tiling that directly inspired :person:`Roger Penrose's <penrose>` aperiodic P1 tiling (:cite:`1974 <penrose:1974>`).
