Proposition XXVII: Summary
==========================

The Two Isohedral Rhombic Zonohedra
-----------------------------------

Kepler proves that exactly two convex isohedral polyhedra are bounded by congruent non-square rhombi:

* **Rhombic Dodecahedron** (:math:`F=12, V=14, E=24`, diagonal ratio :math:`\sqrt{2}:1`, dihedral :math:`120^\circ`, space-filling): dual of the cuboctahedron.
* **Rhombic Triacontahedron** (:math:`F=30, V=32, E=60`, diagonal ratio :math:`\phi:1`, dihedral :math:`144^\circ`): dual of the icosidodecahedron.
