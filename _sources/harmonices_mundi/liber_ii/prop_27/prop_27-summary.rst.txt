Proposition XXVII: Summary
==========================

The Two Isohedral Rhombic Zonohedra
-----------------------------------

Kepler proves that exactly two convex isohedral polyhedra are bounded by congruent non-square rhombi:

* **Rhombic Dodecahedron** ($F=12, V=14, E=24$, diagonal ratio $\sqrt{2}:1$, dihedral $120^\circ$, space-filling): dual of the cuboctahedron.
* **Rhombic Triacontahedron** ($F=30, V=32, E=60$, diagonal ratio $\phi:1$, dihedral $144^\circ$): dual of the icosidodecahedron.
