Proposition XXVII: Notes & Provenance
=====================================

Transcription & Provenance
--------------------------

* **Primary Witness**: Johannes Kepler, *Harmonices Mundi Libri V*, Liber II, Propositio XXVII (:cite:`Linz: Tampach/Plancus, 1619, p. 61 <kepler:1619:hm>`; :cite:`ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. V, p. 141 <frisch:1864:v5>`).
* **Folio Scans**: Verified against 300-DPI scan ``harmonices_mundi:0085`` (p. 61, featuring Kepler's in-text woodcuts of both rhombic polyhedra) and copperplate ``harmonices_mundi:0081`` (Plates ``Vv`` and ``Xx``).
* **English Track**: Original GEOMETOR English translation.

Link to *Strena* (1611)
-----------------------

Kepler's marginal note on p. 61 ties the Rhombic Dodecahedron directly to his 1611 monograph *Strena seu de Nive Sexangula* (honeybee cell geometry) and introduces the Golden Rhombic Triacontahedron as the thirty-faced golden-section companion verified in ``geometor.kepler.strena_crystallography``.
