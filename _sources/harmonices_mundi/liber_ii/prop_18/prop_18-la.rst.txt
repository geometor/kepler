Propositio XVIII — Tria Sola Plana Uniformia Ex Una Specie
==========================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0069
   :page: p. 51

.. rst-class:: latin-source

*Planum locum perfectissime explent, Plana ejusdem figurae tantum Tria, Trigoni seni, Tetragoni quaterni, Hexagoni terni.* Huc refer Rhombos, constantes ex binis Trigonis Regularibus singulos, & stellas ex Dodecagono sexangulas, exsectis senis radiis.
