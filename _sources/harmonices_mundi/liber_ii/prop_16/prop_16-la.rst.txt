Propositio XVI — Summa Angulorum in Plano 4 Recti, in Solido Minor
==================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0068
   :page: pp. 50–51

.. rst-class:: latin-source

*Angulorum, in plano congruentium, summa semper est 4 rectorum, nunquam major: congruentium in solido est minor hac summa.* Adductis duobus lateribus circa hiatum, & excluso hiatu, necesse est assurgere angulum & solidari.
