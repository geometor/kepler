Proposition XXI: Four or More Species Never Meet Uniformly
==========================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0073
   :page: p. 57

Plane figures of **four or more distinct species** can never meet around a single vertex to fill a planar point, much less rise into a uniform solid. For even the four smallest regular angles—triangle ($60^\circ$), square ($90^\circ$), pentagon ($108^\circ$), and hexagon ($120^\circ$)—already sum to $378^\circ > 360^\circ$, exceeding four right angles.
