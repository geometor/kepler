Proposition XXV: Notes & Provenance
===================================

Transcription & Provenance
--------------------------

* **Primary Witness**: Johannes Kepler, *Harmonices Mundi Libri V*, Liber II, Propositio XXV (:cite:`Linz: Tampach/Plancus, 1619, pp. 57–60 <kepler:1619:hm>`; :cite:`ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. V, pp. 137–140 <frisch:1864:v5>`).
* **Folio Scans**: Transcribed directly from 300-DPI scan ``harmonices_mundi:0073`` (p. 57) and illustrated on the copperplate ``harmonices_mundi:0081`` (figures ``Oo``, ``Pp``, ``Qq``, ``Rr``, plus the five element-emblematic Platonic solids).
* **English Track**: Original GEOMETOR English translation.

Relation to Euclid and Mysterium Cosmographicum
-----------------------------------------------

Kepler explicitly characterizes Proposition XXV as a *scholium* to Euclid's closing theorem (:just:`XIII.18`) and links the five regular solids directly back to his 1596 cosmological nesting in *Mysterium Cosmographicum* (marginal cross-reference on p. 60: *"Respice ad schema lib. V cap. III"*).
