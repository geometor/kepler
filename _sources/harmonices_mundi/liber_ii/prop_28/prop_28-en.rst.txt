Proposition XXVIII: The Thirteen Archimedean Solids
===================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0086
   :page: p. 62

**Of perfect solid congruences of the lower degree there are thirteen species, from which arise the thirteen Archimedean Bodies.**

Cleanly setting aside the two infinite families of regular **prisms** ($4.4.n$) and **antiprisms** ($3.3.3.n$), Kepler reconstructs, names, and proves the completeness of all thirteen semi-regular convex solids (:term:`corpora semiregularia Archimedis <archimedean-solids>`) recorded by :person:`Pappus <pappus>` (:cite:`Synagoge V.19 <pappus:synagoge:v>`):

1. **Truncated Tetrahedron**
2. **Cuboctahedron** (*Figure 8*, $3.4.3.4$)
3. **Truncated Cube**
4. **Truncated Octahedron**
5. **Rhombicuboctahedron** (*Figure 10*, $3.4.4.4$)
6. **Great Rhombicuboctahedron** (Truncated Cuboctahedron)
7. **Snub Cube** (*Cubus simus*, *Figure 12*, $3.3.3.3.4$)
8. **Icosidodecahedron** (*Figure 9*, $3.5.3.5$)
9. **Truncated Dodecahedron**
10. **Truncated Icosahedron**
11. **Rhombicosidodecahedron** (*Figure 11*, $3.4.5.4$)
12. **Great Rhombicosidodecahedron** (Truncated Icosidodecahedron)
13. **Snub Dodecahedron** (*Dodecaedron simum*, *Figure 13*, $3.3.3.3.5$)
