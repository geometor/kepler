Kepler's Enduring Polyhedral Nomenclature
-----------------------------------------

Every modern student of geometry speaks Kepler's Latin daily: *cuboctahedron*, *icosidodecahedron*, *rhombic dodecahedron*, *rhombic triacontahedron*, *truncation*, and *snub* (*simus*—"snub-nosed", for the chiral `3.3.3.3.4` and `3.3.3.3.5` solids) were all coined or standardized in *Harmonices Mundi* Book II (:cite:`Coxeter 1973 <coxeter:1973>`). Notice that Kepler's *Truncated Cuboctahedron* (No. 6, ``4.6.8``) and *Truncated Icosidodecahedron* (No. 7, ``4.6.10``) are explicitly marked by Kepler himself as *"not because they arise by truncation, but because they resemble a truncated Cuboctahedron/Icosidodecahedron."*
