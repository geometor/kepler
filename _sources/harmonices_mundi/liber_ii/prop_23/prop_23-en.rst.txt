Proposition XXIII: Two Angles of an Odd-Sided Figure with One of Another
========================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0073
   :page: p. 57

**Proposition XXIII.**

*Two plane angles of an odd-sided figure, meeting with one of another species, do not form any regular solid.*

For by Proposition XVIII [XVII], the solid angles become diverse in form, contrary to Definitions V to X.
