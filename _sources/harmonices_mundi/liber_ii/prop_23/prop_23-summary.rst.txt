Proposition XXIII: Summary
==========================

Extends Book II's odd-cycle parity theorem (Prop. XVII) from planar tilings to three-dimensional polyhedra: whenever a trivalent vertex has type :math:`p.p.q` with :math:`p` odd, the two :math:`p`-gons sharing an edge force their shared :math:`p`-gon to alternate :math:`p` and :math:`q` around its :math:`p` edges, which fails on an odd cycle. This immediately rules out non-existent vertex types such as `3.3.q` (:math:`q \ne 3`) and `5.5.q` (:math:`q \ne 5`).
