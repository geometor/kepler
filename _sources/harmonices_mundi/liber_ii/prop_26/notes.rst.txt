Proposition XXVI: Notes & Provenance
====================================

Transcription & Provenance
--------------------------

* **Primary Witness**: Johannes Kepler, *Harmonices Mundi Libri V*, Liber II, Propositio XXVI (:cite:`Linz: Tampach/Plancus, 1619, p. 60 <kepler:1619:hm>`; :cite:`ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. V, p. 140 <frisch:1864:v5>`).
* **Folio Scans**: Verified against 300-DPI scans ``harmonices_mundi:0084`` (p. 60, *XXVI. Propositio*) and copperplate ``harmonices_mundi:0081`` (Plates ``Ss`` and ``Tt``).
* **English Track**: Original GEOMETOR English translation.

Geometric Context
-----------------

Kepler observes that both star polyhedra emerge by extending the planar faces of the convex dodecahedron and icosahedron, leaving the regular pentagon as an interior "heart" (*cor*) inside each pentagrammic face. Both solids are rendered interactively in exact :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\phi)` coordinates above.
