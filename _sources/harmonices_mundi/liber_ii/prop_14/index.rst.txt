Proposition XIV: At Least Three Plane Angles Meet in Planar Congruence
======================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0068
   :page: p. 50

**Proposition XIV.**

*No fewer than three plane angles are congruent in a plane.*

For around any point of meeting lies the sum of four right angles; yet the angle of no figure equals the sum of two right angles; therefore any two angles whatsoever are still less than four right angles; two therefore do not fill out a plane, by Definition I.
