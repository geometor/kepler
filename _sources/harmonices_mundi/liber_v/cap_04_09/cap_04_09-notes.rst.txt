Capita IV–IX: Historical & Mathematical Commentary
==================================================

Angular Momentum & the Inverse-Square Ratio of Apsidal Angular Speeds
---------------------------------------------------------------------

In *Astronomia Nova* (:cite:`1609 <kepler:1609:an>`), Kepler discovered the Second Law (equal focal areas in equal times, $dA/dt = \frac{1}{2} r^2 \dot{\theta} = \text{const}$). At perihelion $r_{\text{per}} = a(1 - e)$ and aphelion $r_{\text{aph}} = a(1 + e)$, the velocity vector is perpendicular to the solar radius vector, so the heliocentric angular speeds $\omega = \dot{\theta}$ satisfy **strictly**

$$\frac{\omega_{\text{per}}}{\omega_{\text{aph}}} = \left(\frac{r_{\text{aph}}}{r_{\text{per}}}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{1 + e}{1 - e}\right)^2.$$

Solving $\left(\frac{1 + e}{1 - e}\right)^2 = \frac{q}{p}$ for a rational harmonic interval $p : q$ yields the algebraic eccentricity law $e = \frac{\sqrt{q} - \sqrt{p}}{\sqrt{q} + \sqrt{p}}$. For Mars ($p : q = 2 : 3$), this gives $e = \frac{\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{2}} = 5 - 2\sqrt{6} \approx 0.1010$, remarkably close to Mars's true orbital eccentricity $e \approx 0.0934$!
