Chapter VII: Notes & Provenance
===============================

Folio span runs from the bottom of ``0299`` (p. 207) through ``0304`` (p. 212, misprinted ``213`` in the 1619 edition), centered on the full-page musical plates ``0301.g1.png`` (p. 209) and ``0302.g1.png`` (p. 210), plus the five-planet and four-planet tables on pp. 211–212.

Editorial & Musical Notes
-------------------------

* **Motet Challenge (fol. 0300, p. 208)**: In the left margin, Kepler challenges contemporary composers to set a six-voice motet to a text from the Psalms, noting that the Moon sings a separate monody beside the Earth.
* **Creation Chords (pp. 209–210)**: Four six-voice chords (*Genus Durum*: ``Ut ♮`` / ``Ut c``; *Genus Molle*: ``Ut ♭`` / ``Ut c``).
