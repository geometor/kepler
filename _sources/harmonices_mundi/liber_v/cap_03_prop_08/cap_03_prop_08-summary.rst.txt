Caput III Prop. VIII: Executive Geometric Summary
=================================================

Kepler's Third Harmonic Law ($T^2 = a^3$)
-----------------------------------------

In *Harmonices Mundi* Book V, Chapter III, Proposition VIII, Kepler formulates the universal mathematical law linking planetary orbital periods to mean heliocentric distances: the squares of the periodic times are proportional to the cubes of the semi-major axes ($(T_1/T_2)^2 = (a_1/a_2)^3$).
