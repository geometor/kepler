Chapter V: Summary
==================

Demonstrates how the Sun's view of planetary speeds maps the six planets' aphelion and perihelion angular velocities onto Book III's Just-Intonation diatonic lattice, partitioning the octave into the complete eight-note scales of *Cantus Durus* (:math:`G, A, \natural, c, d, e, f\sharp, g`) and *Cantus Molle* (:math:`G, A, b, c, d, e\flat, f, g`) over exact 5-limit monochord denominators (`2160–864`, directly matching Book III Chapter VI, ``0297.g1.png``).
