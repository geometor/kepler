Caput VII — Proportio Omnium Octo Sonorum Usualium Unius Diapason
=================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0134
   :page: pp. 44–46

.. rst-class:: latin-source

*Proportio omnium octo sonorum usualium unius diapason.* Expressas vides in numeris minimis proportionum octo longitudines in Cantu duro (`360, 405, 432, 480, 540, 576, 640, 720`).
