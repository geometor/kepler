Prooemium — De Causis Harmonicarum Proportionum & Ptolemaei Harmonica
=====================================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0092
   :page: pp. 1–9

.. rst-class:: latin-source

*Harmonices Mundi Liber III, in quo agitur de Origine Proportionum Harmonicarum, deque Natura & Differentiis rerum ad Cantum pertinentium.* Hactenus figurarum geometria tradita est; nunc ex circuli divisionibus per figuras scibiles veras consonantiarum causas exquiremus, rejicientes nudas Pythagoreorum numerorum abstractiones.
