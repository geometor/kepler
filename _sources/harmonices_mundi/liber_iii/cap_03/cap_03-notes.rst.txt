Chapter III: Notes & Provenance
===============================

Begins midway down folio ``0118`` (p. 28) and continues through the upper half of ``0121`` (p. 31), ending directly before ``CAPUT IV.``.
Directly cited in Chapters VI and XIV.

* **Printed Figures & Marginalia**: Folios ``0118``–``0120`` contain marginal notes (*Modus constituendi...*, *Elenchus hujusmodi*, *Bina Medietas Harmonica*, *De Trinitate concordantiarum*), inline mensural staves, and the permutation table (*Ordo Proportionum Consonarum minimarum*).

--------------------------
