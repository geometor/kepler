Chapter III: Summary
====================

Rejects the ancients' arithmetic rule for harmonic means, deriving the six consonant pairs dividing the octave (:math:`3:4:5:6` through :math:`15:20:24:30`), establishing the triadic basis of polyphony (:math:`4:5:6` and :math:`10:12:15`), and explaining why three-part harmony arises from plane geometric causes rather than solid dimensions or mystical numerology.
