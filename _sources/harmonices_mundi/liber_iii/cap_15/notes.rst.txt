Chapter XV: Notes & Provenance
==============================

In the 1619 print, marginal ``CAP. XV.`` appears prematurely on ``0163`` (p. 73), while ``CAPUT XV.`` begins on ``0164`` (p. 74) and concludes at the top of ``0170`` (p. 80).

Transcription & Provenance
--------------------------

* **Latin Witness**: *Public-domain Neo-Latin text from Ioannis Keppleri Harmonices Mundi* (:cite:`Linz: Tampach/Plancus, 1619, pp. 74–80 <kepler:1619:hm>`; :cite:`ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. V, pp. 165–240 <frisch:1864:v5>`).
* **English Track**: *Original GEOMETOR English translation & critical apparatus* (:cite:`Linz, 1619, pp. 74–80 <kepler:1619:hm>`).
* **Folio Scan**: Verified 300-DPI primary scans ``harmonices_mundi:0164``–``0170`` (pp. 74–80).

Editorial Notes
---------------

* **Triad & Semitone Geometry**: Links modal *ethos* to whether the Major (*Genus Durum*) or Minor (*Genus Molle*) third sits above the final, and whether the :math:`15:16` semitone rises or falls.
* **Marginal Diagram (p. 77)**: Illustrates alternating Fibonacci rectangles (:math:`1, 2, 3, 5, 8`) beside the divine-proportion series.
