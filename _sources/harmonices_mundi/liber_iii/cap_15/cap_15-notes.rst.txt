Chapter XV: Notes & Provenance
==============================

In the 1619 print, a marginal marker ``CAP. XV.`` appears prematurely on ``0163`` (p. 73), while the true display heading ``CAPUT XV.`` stands cleanly at the top of ``0164`` (p. 74).
