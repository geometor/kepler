Proposition XXI: Notes & Provenance
===================================

Spans the bottom of folio ``0030`` (p. 12) onto the top of folio ``0031`` (p. 13).

In Euclid's *Elements* Book X, Propositions 66–70 (for additive irrationals) and 103–107 (for subtractive irrationals) establish that any line commensurable in length with one of the twelve compound irrational species belongs to the same species—making each Euclidean class closed under rational scaling and eliminating redundant combinations from Kepler's hierarchy.
