Definition XIV: Third Degree (Expressible in Square Only)
=========================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0027
   :page: p. 10

The **third degree** is when a line is inexpressible in length yet its **square is expressible**, belonging to the second degree. It is called in Greek *ῥητὴ δυνάμει*, **expressible in square** (:term:`effabilis potentiâ tantùm <effabilis-potentia>`)—such as the side of the **Square** ($s_4^2 = \frac{1}{2}D^2$) and **Equilateral Triangle** ($s_3^2 = \frac{3}{4}D^2$).
