Preface: Summary
================

Purpose of Euclid Book X Recovered
----------------------------------

Against :person:`Petrus Ramus <ramus>` and :person:`Willebrord Snellius <snellius>`, :person:`Kepler <kepler>` defends :person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s Tenth Book and :person:`Proclus <proclus>`'s commentary on *Elements* Book I. Kepler argues that geometric figuration and proportion are fundamentally mental and archetypal acts, that Euclid's classification of irrational magnitudes (*medials*, *binomials*, *apotomes*) exists specifically to distinguish constructible regular polygons (*scibilia*) from non-constructible ones (*inscibilia*), and that the five Platonic solids form the architectural telos of the *Elements* just as they govern planetary intervals in his *Mysterium Cosmographicum*.
