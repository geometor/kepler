Prooemium: Executive Geometric Summary
======================================

Purpose of Euclid Book X Recovered
----------------------------------

Against :person:`Petrus Ramus <ramus>`, :person:`Kepler <kepler>` identifies the architectural telos of Euclid's classification of quadratic irrationals: establishing a rigorous hierarchy of **degrees of geometric knowability** (:term:`scibilitas <scibilitas>`) that separates constructible regular polygons from non-constructible ones.
