Definition XV: Summary
======================

Degree IV handles fourth-root radicals $\sqrt{\sqrt{q}} D = q^{1/4} D$, whose squares $\sqrt{q} D^2$ (*Meson* areas) appear naturally in the areas of inscribed regular octagons and dodecagons.
