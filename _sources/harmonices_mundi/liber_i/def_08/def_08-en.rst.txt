Definition VIII: Knowable (Scibilis) and the Eight Degrees
==========================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0025
   :page: p. 8

**Knowable** (:term:`scibile <scibilitas>`, Greek γνώριμον) is said of that which is either immediately measurable by the diameter itself (for a line) or by its square (for a surface), or at least formed from such quantities by a **definite and geometrical chain of ratios**—however long the sequence—so that it ultimately depends upon the diameter or its square. Conversely, lines that can be described by some curve or mechanical motion yet lack any such chain to the diameter—like the regular heptagon—remain **unknowable** (*inscibilia*).

From the diameter $D = 2R$, Kepler descends through **eight ordered degrees of geometric knowability** (:term:`Degrees II–VIII <mizon-elasson>`), pairing the constructive triumph of the **Regular Pentagon** (:g:`PENT5`, :term:`Apotome <binominis-apotome>` / *Elasson*, :just:`XIII.9`–:just:`XIII.10`) against the irreducible cubic obstruction of the **Regular Heptagon**:
