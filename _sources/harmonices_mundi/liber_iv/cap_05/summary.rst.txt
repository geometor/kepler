Chapter V: Summary
==================

In the geometric heart of Book IV, Kepler applies both *Scibilitas* (Book I) and *Congruentia* (Book II) to explain why precisely eight celestial aspects are efficacious—Opposition (:math:`180^\circ`), Trine (:math:`120^\circ`), Square (:math:`90^\circ`), Sextile (:math:`60^\circ`), Quintile (:math:`72^\circ`), Biquintile (:math:`144^\circ`), Semisextile (:math:`30^\circ`), and Quincunx (:math:`150^\circ`) alongside the weaker Octile/Trioctile (:math:`45^\circ, 135^\circ`) and Decile/Tridecile (:math:`36^\circ, 108^\circ`)—anchored by archival woodcuts ``0225.g1.png`` and ``0239.g1.png``.
