Praeambulum: Metaphysical & Astrological Scope of Book IV
=========================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0196
   :page: pp. 105–107

Opening **Book IV** (*Metaphysicus, Psychologicus & Astrologicus*), Kepler shifts from audible musical sound to the **celestial rays descending upon Earth**. Does harmony exist solely in vibrating strings, or do the **planetary aspects**—Conjunction, Opposition, Square, Trine, Sextile, Quintile, Biquintile—act upon sublunary souls and Earth's atmosphere by purely geometric resonance?
