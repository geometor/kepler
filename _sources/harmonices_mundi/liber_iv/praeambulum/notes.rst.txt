Praeambulum: Notes & Provenance
===============================

* **Folio Span**: Title folio on ``0195`` (p. 105); *Praeambulum & Ratio Ordinis* on ``0196``–``0197`` (pp. 106–107), concluding with the *Capita Libri IV* table of contents.
* **Marginalia (fol. 0196, p. 106)**: Kepler adds a marginal gloss citing Socrates in Plato's *Republic* Book VII (referenced via Proclus, *In Euclidem* I): *"Atqui Socrates apud Platonem, citante Proclo lib. 1 in Eucl., Musicum illum definit, qui ab iis Harmoniis quae insunt in sensu, progreditur ad insensiles harmonias, proportionesque earum investigandas. Qui tandem evadit ipsi in Philosophum, expergefacta harmonia, ad contemplandum Primum verum Ens, veritatemque ipsam."* (Socrates defines the true musician as one who advances from sensible harmonies to intellectual harmonies and their ratios, awakening as a philosopher to contemplate the First True Being.)
* **Editorial Note**: In *Praeambulum*, Kepler explicitly flags that his critique of Ptolemy's *Harmonics* Book III is deferred to the Appendix at the end of Book V.
