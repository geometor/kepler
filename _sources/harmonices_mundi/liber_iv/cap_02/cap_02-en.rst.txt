Chapter II: Faculties of the Soul According to Harmonies
========================================================

.. folio:: harmonices_mundi:0210
   :page: pp. 120–125

Chapter II classifies the **vegetative, sensitive, and intellectual faculties of the Soul**—and the **World Soul / Earth Soul** (*Anima Telluris*)—demonstrating how each faculty responds instinctively to pure circular proportions without conscious discursive calculation (just as a peasant feels rhythmic cadence or Earth trembles into storms under harmonic conjunctions).
