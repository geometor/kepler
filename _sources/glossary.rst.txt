Latin & Mathematical Glossary
=============================

Lexicon of the **20** foundational Neo-Latin, Greek, and symbolic-geometry terms across *Mysterium Cosmographicum*, *Strena*, and *Harmonices Mundi*.

Epistemology & Euclidean Irrationals (*Harmonices Mundi* I)
-----------------------------------------------------------

.. _term-scibilitas:

* **scibilitas (knowability / demonstrabilitas)** — Kepler's criterion in *Harmonices Mundi* Book I (Defs. VII–VIII): a chord or polygon side is *scibilis* when constructible from the circle's diameter in finitely many Euclidean steps and expressible within the quadratic radical hierarchy of Euclid Book X.

.. _term-inscibilitas:

* **inscibilitas (geometric non-constructibility)** — Impossibility of straightedge-and-compass determination from the diameter, exemplified by the regular heptagon (*Septangulum*, Prop. XLV), whose algebraic chord equation is an irreducible cubic.

.. _term-effabilis-longitudine:

* **effabilis longitudine (ῥητὴ μήκει)** — Commensurable in length with the rational diameter :math:`\mathbb{Q}`, such as the regular hexagon's side :math:`s_6 = R = \frac{1}{2}D`.

.. _term-effabilis-potentia:

* **effabilis potentiâ tantùm (ῥητὴ δυνάμει μόνον)** — Incommensurable in length, yet commensurable in square (*quadrance*), such as the inscribed square (:math:`s_4^2 = 2R^2`) and equilateral triangle (:math:`s_3^2 = 3R^2`).

.. _term-mese:

* **mese (μέση / medial line)** — Geometric mean of two lines commensurable in square only (Euclid X.21).

.. _term-binominis-apotome:

* **binominis & apotome (ἐκ δύο ὀνομάτων & ἀποτομή)** — Sum (:math:`a + \sqrt{b}`) and difference (:math:`a - \sqrt{b}`) of lines commensurable in square only; the regular decagon's side :math:`s_{10} = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}R` is an apotome.

.. _term-mizon-elasson:

* **mizon & elasson (μείζων & ἐλάσσων)** — Major (:math:`\sqrt{a + \sqrt{b}}`) and minor (:math:`\sqrt{a - \sqrt{b}}`) nested radicals (Euclid X.39, X.76); the pentagram chord is a *mizon* line and the pentagon side :math:`s_5 = \frac{R}{2}\sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}}` is an *elasson* line.

.. _term-quadrance:

* **potentia / quadrance** (:math:`Q = r^2`) — Exact squared segment length (Euclid's δύναμις / *potentia*; Rational Trigonometry's *quadrance*), eliminating unnecessary nested square roots across polyhedral sphere ratios.

Divine Proportion, Polyhedra & Packing (*Mysterium*, *Strena*, *Harmonices* II)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. _term-sectio-divina:

* **sectio divina / proportio divina (extrema et media ratio)** — Division :math:`y : x = x : (x + y)` yielding :math:`\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}` with :math:`\phi^2 = \phi + 1`; celebrated by Kepler as the "precious jewel" of geometry and self-propagating archetype of pentamerous growth.

.. _term-kepler-triangle:

* **triangulum rectangulum Kepleri (Kepler Right Triangle)** — Unique right triangle with sides :math:`(1, \sqrt{\phi}, \phi)` and quadrances :math:`(1, \phi, \phi^2)` in continuous geometric proportion over :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\phi})` (*Epistola ad Mästlinum*, Oct 1597).

.. _term-corpora-quinque-regularia:

* **corpora quinque regularia (Five Platonic Solids)** — Cube :math:`\{4,3\}`, Tetrahedron :math:`\{3,3\}`, and Dodecahedron :math:`\{5,3\}` (primary outer solids), together with Icosahedron :math:`\{3,5\}` and Octahedron :math:`\{3,4\}` (secondary inner solids).

.. _term-circulus-mensor:

* **Terra est Circulus mensor omnium (Earth as Measuring Orb)** — Kepler's cosmological rule placing Earth's unit orbit (:math:`R = 1`) between the three outer primary solids and two inner secondary solids (*Mysterium* Cap. XIV).

.. _term-dodecaedron-rhombicum:

* **dodecaedron rhombicum (Rhombic Dodecahedron)** — Isohedral 12-rhombus space-filling Voronoi polyhedron of the FCC lattice with diagonal ratio :math:`\sqrt{2}:1` and dihedral angle :math:`120^\circ` (*Strena*, 1611).

.. _term-triacontaedron-rhombicum:

* **triacontaedron rhombicum (Rhombic Triacontahedron)** — Isohedral 30-rhombus golden zonohedron with diagonal ratio :math:`\phi:1`, 32 vertices, 60 edges, and dihedral angle :math:`144^\circ` (*Strena*, 1611; *Harmonices* II & V, 1619).

.. _term-kepler-conjecture:

* **coacervatio globulorum densissima (Kepler Sphere-Packing Conjecture)** — Assertion in *Strena* (1611) that face-centered cubic and hexagonal close-packings (kissing number 12, density :math:`\frac{\pi}{3\sqrt{2}} \approx 74.048\%`) achieve the maximum possible density of congruent spheres in :math:`\mathbb{R}^3`.

.. _term-congruentia:

* **congruentia figurarum (Planar & Solid Congruence)** — Edge-to-edge fitting of regular and star polygons summing to :math:`360^\circ` in the plane (11 uniform Archimedean tilings) or closing around solid vertices (:math:`< 360^\circ`) in 3-space (*Harmonices* Book II).

.. _term-archimedean-solids:

* **corpora semiregularia Archimedis (Thirteen Archimedean Solids)** — The 13 convex vertex-transitive semi-regular polyhedra reconstructed and proved complete by Kepler in *Harmonices Mundi* Book II Prop. XXVIII.

.. _term-stella-quinquangula:

* **stella quinquangula & corpora stellata (Pentagram & Stellated Dodecahedra)** — Regular star pentagon :math:`\{5/2\}` and Kepler's Small (:math:`\{5/2, 5\}`) and Great (:math:`\{5/2, 3\}`) Stellated Dodecahedra.

Celestial Harmonics (*Harmonices Mundi* V)
------------------------------------------

.. _term-harmonia-extremorum:

* **harmonia motuum extremorum (Aphelion–Perihelion Consonance)** — Exact just-intonation musical intervals formed by planets' extremal daily heliocentric angular speeds (Saturn :math:`4:5`, Jupiter :math:`5:6`, Mars :math:`2:3`, Earth :math:`15:16`, Venus :math:`24:25`, Mercury :math:`5:12`).

.. _term-lex-tertia:

* **proportio sesquialtera / Lex Tertia (Kepler's Harmonic Third Law)** — Fundamental celestial law (*Harmonices Mundi* Book V, Cap. III, Prop. VIII): orbital periods stand in the exact :math:`3/2` power ratio of semi-major axes, :math:`(T_1/T_2)^2 = (a_1/a_2)^3`.
