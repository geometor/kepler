Tycho Brahe
===========

Imperial observational astronomer at Prague whose decades of high-precision planetary observations provided the empirical foundation for Kepler's discovery of the elliptical orbits (*Astronomia Nova*, 1609) and the planetary harmonic consonances (*Harmonices Mundi*, 1619).
