===============
Johannes Kepler
===============

The works of :person:`Johannes Kepler <kepler>` (1571–1630) revolutionized modern astronomy and geometry, uniting Copernican cosmology with the Platonic solids, founding mathematical crystallography, and discovering the harmonic laws of planetary motion. Within the interconnected `GEOMETOR <https://geometor.com/books/>`_ semantic digital archive, the *Mysterium Cosmographicum*, *Strena seu de Nive Sexangula*, and *Harmonices Mundi* are liberated from static print bounds—decomposed into addressable semantic units, calibrated vector diagrams, primary-source folio provenance, and cross-corpus citations speaking one shared geometric language.

Interactive 3D Polyhedra & Calibrated Plates
--------------------------------------------

* **3D Nested Platonic Polyhedra** (*Mysterium Cosmographicum*, Tabula III & Chapter XIV): explore Kepler's five nested regular solids between the six heliocentric planetary orbs in the :ref:`Interactive 3D Nested Polyhedra Studio <mysterium-3d-studio>` (also paired with Tabula III in :ref:`Chapter II <mc-cap_02-en>` and Tabella IIII in :ref:`Chapter XIV <mc-cap_14-en>`).
* **3D Kepler–Poinsot Star Polyhedra** (*Harmonices Mundi*, Book II, Prop. XXVI): rotate the Small and Great Stellated Dodecahedra ($\{5/2, 5\}$ and $\{5/2, 3\}$) beside Kepler's 1619 engravings ``Ss`` and ``Tt`` in :ref:`Proposition XXVI <hm-liber-ii-prop_26-en>`.
* **Paired Archival Bitmaps & Vectorizations**: compare Kepler's original 1596/1611/1619 copperplates and woodcuts side by side with interactive theme-adaptive ``.euclid-graphic`` vectorizations beginning with **Tabella I & II** in :ref:`Chapter I <mc-cap_01-en>`.
