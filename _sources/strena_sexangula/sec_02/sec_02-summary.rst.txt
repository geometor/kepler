Honeycombs & Rhombic Polyhedra: Summary
=======================================

Discovery of the Two Isohedral Rhombic Zonohedra
------------------------------------------------

Extending :person:`Pappus's <pappus>` planar honeycomb analysis into three dimensions via the trihedral rhombic keel of opposing bee cells, :person:`Kepler <kepler>` discovered both vertex-dual Catalanesque rhombic polyhedra:

.. list-table:: Kepler's Two Isohedral Rhombic Polyhedra (``geometor.kepler.strena_packing``)
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 24 14 18 18 26

   * - Polyhedron
     - :math:`F / V / E`
     - Diagonal Ratio
     - Dihedral Angle
     - Dual / Space-Filling Property
   * - **Rhombic Dodecahedron** (*Dodecaedron Rhombicum*)
     - :math:`12 / 14 / 24`
     - :math:`\sqrt{2} : 1` (:math:`Q = 2`)
     - :math:`120^\circ` (:math:`\cos\theta = -\frac{1}{2}`)
     - Dual of Cuboctahedron; **3D Voronoi space-filler** (:math:`3 \times 120^\circ = 360^\circ`)
   * - **Rhombic Triacontahedron** (*Triacontaedron Rhombicum*)
     - :math:`30 / 32 / 60`
     - :math:`\phi : 1` (:math:`Q = \phi + 1`)
     - :math:`144^\circ` (:math:`\cos\theta = -\frac{\phi}{2}`)
     - Dual of Icosidodecahedron; golden icosahedral zonohedron
