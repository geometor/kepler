Chapter II: Executive Geometric Summary
=======================================

Curved, Straight & The Solid Angle Obstruction
----------------------------------------------

In Chapter II, :person:`Kepler <kepler>` invokes :person:`Nicholas of Cusa <cusanus>` to establish the fundamental cosmological polarity between the spherical universe (*Curvum*, likeness of God) and interior polyhedral boundaries (*Rectum*, likeness of creatures). By Euclid XIII.18, solid angle closure requires face angles summing to strictly less than $360^\circ$, admitting precisely the five Platonic solids:

* Three regular equilateral triangle vertices ($180^\circ, 240^\circ, 300^\circ$): **Tetrahedron**, **Octahedron**, **Icosahedron**;
* One square vertex ($270^\circ$): **Cube**;
* One regular pentagon vertex ($324^\circ$): **Dodecahedron**.
