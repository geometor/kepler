Algebraic Note
--------------

Kepler's distinction between the two "treasures" anticipates the field-extension tower of Euclidean constructibility: the first three Platonic solids live inside quadratic extensions $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3})$, whereas the Dodecahedron and Icosahedron enter the golden field $\mathbb{Q}(\phi) = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$.
