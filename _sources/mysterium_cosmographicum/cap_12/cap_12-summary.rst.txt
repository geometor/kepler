Chapters XII–XIII: Executive Geometric Summary
==============================================

The Two Treasures & Polar-Duality Sphere Ratios
-----------------------------------------------

1. **The Two Great Treasures of Geometry**: In Chapter XII, :person:`Kepler <kepler>` formulates his enduring aphorism pairing the **Pythagorean Theorem** (*measure of gold*, governing the Cube, Tetrahedron, and Octahedron over $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})$) with the **Extreme and Mean Ratio** (:term:`sectio divina <sectio-divina>` $\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$, *precious jewel*, governing the Dodecahedron and Icosahedron over $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$).
2. **Polar-Duality Insphere/Circumsphere Invariance**: Dual regular solids exchange vertices ($V$) and faces ($F$) while preserving edge count ($E$) and exact insphere-to-circumsphere quadrance ratios:
