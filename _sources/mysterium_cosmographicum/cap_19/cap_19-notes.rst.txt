Chapter XIX: Notes & Provenance
===============================

Mästlin's Role in the 1596 Edition & The Venus Anomaly
------------------------------------------------------

Folio ``0081`` (p. 69) preserves :person:`Michael Mästlin's <maestlin>` direct voice inside Kepler's text—confirming how closely teacher and pupil collaborated during the Tübingen printing. Historically, the $2^\circ 18'$ residual on Venus proved real rather than observational: in *Harmonices Mundi* Book V (:cite:`1619 <kepler:1619:hm>`), Kepler finally explained why Venus's aphelion/perihelion shell departs slightly from the pure Icosahedron through the musical consonances of extremal angular velocities.
