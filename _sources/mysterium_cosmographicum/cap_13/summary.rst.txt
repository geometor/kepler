Chapter XIII: Summary
=====================

Polar-Duality Insphere & Circumsphere Ratios
--------------------------------------------

Kepler computes the exact insphere-to-circumsphere ratios for all five Platonic solids normalized to a circumradius :math:`R_{\text{out}} = 1000`, using the Pythagorean right triangle :math:`HO^2 - HI^2 = OI^2` (:just:`I.47`) and Euclidean edge constructions from Book XIII (and Candalla's Book XIV–XV additions). Dual regular solids exchange vertices (:math:`V`) and faces (:math:`F`) while preserving edge count (:math:`E`) and exact insphere-to-circumsphere quadrance ratios:

.. list-table:: Exact Platonic Sphere Ratios (``geometor.kepler.mysterium_spheres``)
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 18 12 18 26 26

   * - Solid
     - Schläfli
     - Planetary Pair
     - Exact Quadrance Ratio :math:`Q_{\text{in}} / Q_{\text{out}}`
     - Exact Radius Ratio :math:`r_{\text{in}} / R_{\text{out}}` (Kepler 1596: :math:`r_{\text{in}}` for :math:`R=1000`)
   * - **Cube**
     - :math:`\{4, 3\}`
     - Saturn :math:`\to` Jupiter
     - :math:`\frac{1}{3}`
     - :math:`\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \approx 0.57735` (``577``)
   * - **Tetrahedron**
     - :math:`\{3, 3\}`
     - Jupiter :math:`\to` Mars
     - :math:`\frac{1}{9}`
     - :math:`\frac{1}{3} \approx 0.33333` (``333``)
   * - **Dodecahedron**
     - :math:`\{5, 3\}`
     - Mars :math:`\to` Earth
     - :math:`\frac{5 + 2\sqrt{5}}{15} = \frac{4\phi + 3}{15}`
     - :math:`\sqrt{\frac{5 + 2\sqrt{5}}{15}} \approx 0.79465` (``795``)
   * - **Icosahedron**
     - :math:`\{3, 5\}`
     - Earth :math:`\to` Venus
     - :math:`\frac{5 + 2\sqrt{5}}{15} = \frac{4\phi + 3}{15}`
     - :math:`\sqrt{\frac{5 + 2\sqrt{5}}{15}} \approx 0.79465` (``795``)
   * - **Octahedron**
     - :math:`\{3, 4\}`
     - Venus :math:`\to` Mercury
     - :math:`\frac{1}{3}`
     - :math:`\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \approx 0.57735` (``577``; ``707`` for square-section circle)
