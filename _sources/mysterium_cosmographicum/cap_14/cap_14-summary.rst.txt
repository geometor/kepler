Chapter XIV: Summary
====================

Earth as Measuring Boundary (*Circulus mensor omnium*)
------------------------------------------------------

Placing Earth ($R_\oplus = 1$) between the three outer trihedral *primary* solids (Dodecahedron, Tetrahedron, Cube) and the two inner *secondary* solids (Icosahedron, Octahedron) reproduces the qualitative spacing of the six classical planets—especially the vast Jovian gap ($1/3$ tetrahedral ratio between Mars and Jupiter) and tight terrestrial cluster ($0.795$ golden ratios around Earth and Venus).
