Chapter XXII: Why Planets Move Uniformly About the Equant Center
================================================================

.. folio:: mysterium_cosmographicum:0089
   :page: p. 77

.. folio:: mysterium_cosmographicum:0090
   :page: p. 78 (Solar Motive Force & Equant Diagram)

Returning to a subtle technical objection raised by :person:`Michael Mästlin <maestlin>` concerning Chapter XIV, Kepler explains the physical origin of **Ptolemy's equant** (and Copernicus's equivalent small epicyclet): why does a planet move **slower at aphelion** and **faster at perihelion**, sweeping nearly uniform angles around an empty equant point $D$ placed twice as far from the Sun as the orbit's geometric center $B$?

Mästlin had pointed out that Copernicus's tiny epicyclet—introduced to reproduce the equant without breaking uniform circular motion—doubles the formal shell thickness of a planet's orb (see **Tabella IIII**, ``0059``), which would crush the interstitial spaces for the five solids! Kepler replies first that solid shells do not exist (citing Chapter XVI and :person:`Tycho Brahe <brahe>`, *"the noble and most excellent Danish mathematician"*), so only the **actual path swept by the planet's globe** matters.

Even more profoundly, Kepler shows on folio ``0090`` (p. 78) that his **solar moving soul** from Chapter XX directly produces the equant effect inside every single planetary orbit! In his engraved diagram on p. 78:

* Point **$A$** is the source of the motive soul—the **Sun**.
* Point **$B$** is the geometric center of the eccentric planetary path $EFGH$, with eccentricity $AB$.
* Point **$D$** lies along the line of apsides at double distance $AD = 2\,AB$ (so $BD = BA$, and $C$ bisects $BD$).

At aphelion $EF$, the planet stands farther from the Sun $A$ by distance $AB$ than on the concentric circle $NOML$, so the weakened solar force slows it down; at perihelion $HG$, it approaches closer to $A$ by $AB$, so the strengthened solar force accelerates it—making the planet's speed vary inversely with solar distance **exactly as if it moved uniformly about the bisected equant center $D$**! Only Earth's annual motion still puzzled Kepler here, because Copernicus and Ptolemy had given Earth no equant—a puzzle Kepler later cracked in *Astronomia Nova* (:cite:`1609 <kepler:1609:an>`) by proving Earth *does* obey the exact same bisected eccentricity and Area Law!
