Chapter XXII: Notes & Provenance
================================

The Direct Seed of Kepler's Second Law (Equal Areas)
----------------------------------------------------

Folio ``0090`` (p. 78) is arguably the single most prophetic page in the entire *Mysterium Cosmographicum*. Notice three historic milestones converging on one page: (1) Kepler praises :person:`Tycho Brahe <brahe>` by name for dissolving solid celestial spheres; (2) he replaces kinematic equants/epicycles with a **physical radial force law** centered at the true Sun $A$ (inverse-distance speed law $v \propto 1/r$, which is exact at aphelion and perihelion for an ellipse under conservation of angular momentum!); and (3) he flags Earth's missing equant as an unresolved anomaly—the very clue that guided his war on Mars in *Astronomia Nova* (:cite:`1609 <kepler:1609:an>`).
