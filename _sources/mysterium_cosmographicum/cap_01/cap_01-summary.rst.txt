Chapter I: Summary
==================

Physical Realism & The Heliocentrism Defense
--------------------------------------------

Chapter I is Kepler's foundational manifesto of astronomical realism. Against instrumentalists who treated Copernicanism as a convenient fiction, Kepler argues that Copernicus unifies retrograde loops, planetary brightness variations, bounded inner elongations, and interplanetary distance ratios through one single physical motion—Earth's annual orbit—contrasted directly across folding plates **Tabella I** and **Tabella II**.
