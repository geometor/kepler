Chapter I: Notes & Provenance
=============================

Epistemology of Hypotheses & Folding Plates Tabella I–II
--------------------------------------------------------

Kepler's refutation of the *ex falso quodlibet* instrumentalist reading (made famous by Andreas Osiander's anonymous preface to Copernicus's 1543 *De revolutionibus*) marks a decisive watershed in the philosophy of science. By comparing **Tabella I** (``0026``) and **Tabella II** (``0027``), Kepler illustrates why Copernicus could uniquely fix interplanetary proportions—the indispensable empirical precondition for fitting the :term:`five regular solids <corpora-quinque-regularia>` between the six orbs.
