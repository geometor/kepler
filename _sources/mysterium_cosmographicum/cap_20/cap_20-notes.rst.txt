Chapter XX: Notes & Provenance
==============================

From *Anima Motrix* (1596) to *Vis* (1621) & The Third Law (1618)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

In his famous **1621 Author's Note** to Chapter XX (:cite:`Frankfurt, 1621 <kepler:1621:mc2>`), Kepler replaces the word *soul* (*anima*) with physical **force** (*vis*)—writing that if one substitutes "force" for "soul," one obtains the exact principle of celestial physics expounded in *Astronomia Nova* (:cite:`1609 <kepler:1609:an>`)—and triumphantly points out that his 1596 quadratic-like trial law has now been perfected into the exact :term:`sesquialterate proportion <lex-tertia>` $T_1 / T_2 = (a_1 / a_2)^{3/2}$ in *Harmonices Mundi* Book V (:cite:`1619 <kepler:1619:hm>`).

Transcription & Provenance
--------------------------

* **Primary Witness**: Johannes Kepler, *Prodromus Dissertationum Cosmographicarum, continens Mysterium Cosmographicum* (:cite:`Tübingen, 1596, Caput XX, pp. 69–74 (folios 0081–0086) <kepler:1596:mc>`; *Editio altera cum notis authoris*, :cite:`Frankfurt, 1621 <kepler:1621:mc2>`; :cite:`ed. Ch. Frisch, Vol. I, pp. 163–168 <frisch:1858:v1>`).
* **Folio Scans**: Verified 300-DPI scans ``mysterium_cosmographicum:0081``–``0086`` (pp. 69–74). On p. 73 (fol. ``0085``), the 1596 typesetter printed ``275`` opposite Jupiter's motive value ``574`` as a typographical slip for ``572``.
* **English Track**: Direct first-person English translation of the 1596 Latin *editio princeps*.
