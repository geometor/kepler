Chapter IV: Summary
===================

Container vs. Contained & Three-and-Three Planetary Symmetry
------------------------------------------------------------

Kepler explains why the three primary solids lie outside Earth's orbit and the two secondary solids inside: aesthetically, Earth divides the cosmos into three exterior planets (Saturn, Jupiter, Mars) and three interior bodies (Venus, Mercury, and the central Sun); formally, the more fundamental *primary* solids act as containers surrounding Earth while the derivative *secondary* solids are contained within.
