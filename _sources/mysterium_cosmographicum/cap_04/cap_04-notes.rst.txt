Chapter IV: Notes & Provenance
==============================

Teleological vs. Structural Arguments in the 1596 Edition
---------------------------------------------------------

Notice Kepler's intellectual candor on folio ``0038`` (p. 28): he explicitly labels his anthropocentric symmetry argument an *honorarium* (a bonus reflection, not the load-bearing proof), immediately anchoring the assignment on the empirical Copernican ordering of three superior planets (Saturn, Jupiter, Mars) and two inferior planets (Venus, Mercury). In his 1621 *Notae Authoris* (:cite:`Frankfurt, 1621 <kepler:1621:mc2>`), Kepler retains this sober distinction between poetic analogy and geometric necessity.
