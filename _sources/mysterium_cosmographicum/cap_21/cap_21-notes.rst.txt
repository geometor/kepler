Chapter XXI: Notes & Provenance
===============================

Why Kepler's 1596 Period Formula Systematically Overshot Small Gaps
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Look at Kepler's table on folio ``0087`` (p. 75) through modern eyes! Kepler's 1596 algebraic rule effectively used $r_2/r_1 \approx \frac{1}{2}(1 + T_2/T_1)$ (arithmetic mean of $1$ and the period ratio), whereas the true :term:`Third Law <lex-tertia>` is $(T_2/T_1)^{2/3}$. For ratios near $1$ (Mars–Earth, Earth–Venus), $T_1/T_2$ is close to $1$ and the linear approximation systematically overestimates the smaller inner ratio $r_1/r_2$—explaining the exact positive sign pattern (``+36``, ``+43``) that Kepler astutely noticed in 1596!
