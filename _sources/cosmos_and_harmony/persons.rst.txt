.. _kepler-persons:

Index Nominum (Persons)
=======================

Prosopographical register of the **32** geometers, astronomers, natural philosophers, patrons, and editors referenced across **GEOMETOR Kepler**.

Ancient & Medieval Authorities
------------------------------

.. _person-pythagoras:

* **Pythagoras of Samos** (*c. 570 – c. 495 BC*) — Founder of the Pythagorean harmonic and geometric tradition; honored by Kepler alongside the Divine Section as discoverer of the first "great treasure of geometry" (:just:`I.47`).

.. _person-plato:

* **Plato of Athens** (*c. 428 – c. 348 BC*) — Author of the *Timaeus*, associating the regular polyhedra with elemental structure and the dodecahedron with the cosmos.

.. _person-aristotle:

* **Aristotle of Stagira** (*384 – 322 BC*) — Author of *Posterior Analytics*, *De Caelo*, and *Metaphysics*; critically engaged by Kepler on mathematical causality and space-filling polyhedra.

.. _person-euclid:

* **Euclid of Alexandria** (*fl. c. 300 BC*) — Author of the *Elements* (*Stoicheia*), Books I, II, IV, VI, X, and XIII, supplying the demonstrative backbone of *Mysterium Cosmographicum* and *Harmonices Mundi*.

.. _person-archimedes:

* **Archimedes of Syracuse** (*c. 287 – c. 212 BC*) — Discoverer of the thirteen semi-regular convex solids reconstructed and proved complete by Kepler in *Harmonices Mundi* Book II.

.. _person-apollonius:

* **Apollonius of Perga** (*c. 240 – c. 190 BC*) — Author of the *Conics*, whose focal theory of the ellipse enabled Kepler's planetary kinematics.

.. _person-ptolemy:

* **Claudius Ptolemy** (*c. 100 – c. 170 AD*) — Author of the *Almagest* and the *Harmonics*, whose chord geometry and celestial harmonics Kepler transformed onto a heliocentric foundation.

.. _person-pappus:

* **Pappus of Alexandria** (*c. 290 – c. 350 AD*) — Author of the *Synagoge*, Book V of which preserved the hexagonal honeycomb theorem and face counts of the 13 Archimedean solids.

.. _person-proclus:

* **Proclus Diadochus** (*412 – 485 AD*) — Neoplatonic commentator on Euclid Book I, quoted extensively by Kepler on innate archetypal geometry.

.. _person-fibonacci:

* **Leonardo of Pisa (Fibonacci)** (*c. 1170 – c. 1250*) — Author of *Liber Abaci* (1202); Kepler independently formulated the additive law $1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21\dots \to \phi$ in *Strena* (1611).

.. _person-campanus:

* **Campanus of Novara** (*c. 1220 – 1296*) — Medieval Latin commentator on Euclid, noted for star-pentagram angles and Book X irrationals.

Renaissance & Early Modern Contemporaries
-----------------------------------------

.. _person-cusanus:

* **Nicolaus Cusanus (Nicholas of Cusa)** (*1401 – 1464*) — Cardinal and mathematical theologian (*De Docta Ignorantia*, 1440), praised by Kepler as *divinus mihi Cusanus* for contrasting the spherical curved (Creator) with the polyhedral straight (creation).

.. _person-pacioli:

* **Fra Luca Bartolomeo de Pacioli** (*c. 1447 – 1517*) — Author of *De Divina Proportione* (1509), establishing Renaissance terminology of the Divine Proportion.

.. _person-duerer:

* **Albrecht Dürer** (*1471 – 1528*) — Author of *Underweysung der Messung* (1525) on polyhedral nets, pentagonal rosettes, and practical heptagon approximations.

.. _person-copernicus:

* **Nicolaus Copernicus** (*1473 – 1543*) — Author of *De revolutionibus orbium coelestium* (1543), establishing the six heliocentric planetary orbs harmonized by Kepler.

.. _person-rheticus:

* **Georg Joachim Rheticus** (*1514 – 1574*) — Author of *Narratio Prima* (1540), appended by Mästlin to the 1596 *Mysterium Cosmographicum*.

.. _person-ramus:

* **Petrus Ramus (Pierre de la Ramée)** (*1515 – 1572*) — Humanist critic of Euclid Book X, refuted by Kepler in *Harmonices Mundi* Book I Prooemium.

.. _person-brahe:

* **Tycho Brahe** (*1546 – 1601*) — Imperial observational astronomer whose Mars and planetary data anchored both *Astronomia Nova* (1609) and *Harmonices Mundi* Book V (1619).

.. _person-maestlin:

* **Michael Mästlin** (*1550 – 1631*) — Professor of Mathematics at Tübingen, Kepler's mentor, editor of the 1596 *Mysterium*, and recipient of the October 1597 Kepler Right Triangle letter ($1/\phi \approx 0.6180340$).

.. _person-wackher:

* **Johannes Matthäus Wackher von Wackenfels** (*1550 – 1619*) — Imperial Aulic Councillor at Prague and dedicatee of *Strena seu de Nive Sexangula* (1611).

.. _person-buergi:

* **Jost Bürgi** (*1552 – 1632*) — Swiss Imperial clockmaker and Cossic algebraist whose chord equation for the regular heptagon Kepler critiqued in *Harmonices Mundi* Book I Prop. XLV.

.. _person-galileo:

* **Galileo Galilei** (*1564 – 1642*) — Astronomer and physicist whose *Sidereus Nuncius* (1610) discoveries of Jupiter's moons Kepler celebrated and integrated into *Harmonices Mundi* Book V.

.. _person-kepler:

* **Johannes Kepler** (*1571 – 1630*) — Provincial Mathematician at Graz, Imperial Mathematician at Prague, and Mathematician at Linz; author of *Mysterium Cosmographicum* (1596/1621), *Astronomia Nova* (1609), *Strena* (1611), and *Harmonices Mundi* (1619).

.. _person-snellius:

* **Willebrord Snellius** (*1580 – 1626*) — Dutch mathematician at Leiden addressed by Kepler in *Harmonices Mundi* Book I concerning Euclid Book X.

Modern Editors & Mathematical Heirs
-----------------------------------

.. _person-poinsot:

* **Louis Poinsot** (*1777 – 1859*) — Rediscovered Kepler's two stellated dodecahedra in 1809–1810 and discovered their duals.

.. _person-gauss:

* **Carl Friedrich Gauss** (*1777 – 1855*) — Characterized constructible regular $n$-gons via Fermat primes in *Disquisitiones Arithmeticae* (1801).

.. _person-frisch:

* **Christian Frisch** (*1807 – 1881*) — Editor of the eight-volume critical *Joannis Kepleri Astronomi Opera Omnia* (1858–1871).

.. _person-wantzel:

* **Pierre Laurent Wantzel** (*1814 – 1848*) — Proved in 1837 that irreducible cubics (including the heptagon and nonagon) are non-constructible by ruler and compass.

.. _person-thompson:

* **Sir D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson** (*1860 – 1948*) — Author of *On Growth and Form* (1917/1942), extending Kepler's *Strena* into mathematical biology.

.. _person-hoenen:

* **Petrus Hubertus Jacobus Hoenen, S.J.** (*1880 – 1961*) — Author of *De Noetica Geometriae* (1954) in the GEOMETOR canon.

.. _person-coxeter:

* **Harold Scott MacDonald Coxeter** (*1907 – 2003*) — Author of *Regular Polytopes*, systematizing Kepler's uniform tilings, star polyhedra, and zonohedra.

.. _person-penrose:

* **Sir Roger Penrose** (*b. 1931*) — Discoverer of non-periodic pentagonal/decagonal tilings (1974) inspired directly by Kepler's `Figure Aa`.

.. _person-hales:

* **Thomas Callister Hales** (*b. 1958*) — Proved the 1611 Kepler Sphere-Packing Conjecture (1998/2005) and led its Flyspeck formal verification (2014).
